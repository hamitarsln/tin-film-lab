# Critique (art film)

You can read images. Open every still and sheet and look before you score. Be the art director who has to
defend this film next to Anthropic's Fable 5 and Opus 5.5 films, not the author who made it.

## Score 1–10
| Dimension | 8 means |
|---|---|
| Mark legibility | the Tin mark (T, i, n) reads in under half a second, even at 360 px |
| Material truth | the material is a real object or surface with texture, wear and light, not a flat fill |
| Distinctness | it looks nothing like the materials around it (scale, colour, technique, era) |
| Craft | edges, grain, scale and placement feel deliberate; no fringes, no stretched pixels, no blur |
| Tin's world | it sits on Tin's paper and ink and belongs in the same film as the others |
| Story | its era or medium is clear and it sits in the right place in the arc of marketing history |

## Hunt for
Cutout fringes and halos, upscaled mush, a material that only works because of its colour, a mark so busy it
disappears, two materials that read as the same idea, anything that looks like clip art or a filter, modern logos,
people, text in the material that fights the mark.

## Log
Append to `docs/REVIEW_LOG.md`: the round, what you reviewed, scores per still, the weakest three, and what you
changed or dropped. Replace a material outright rather than polishing a weak one.
