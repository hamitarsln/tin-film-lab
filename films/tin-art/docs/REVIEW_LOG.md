# Review log

Rubric: `.claude/skills/critique/SKILL.md`. Each dimension is scored 1–10:
**L** mark legibility (reads in under 0.5 s at 360 px), **M** material truth, **D** distinctness from neighbours,
**C** craft (edges, grain, scale, no fringes or mush), **W** Tin's world (paper, ink, same film), **S** story (era
clear, right place in the arc). The total is out of 60. Every still is judged at full size, on the sheet, and as a
360 px thumbnail.
