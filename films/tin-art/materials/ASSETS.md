  and 2003688927 (1918 Glow Thrift Lamps advertisement) are halftone prints but say "Rights status not evaluated". Skipped.
  Cleveland 1995.199.3 (Camera Work no. 3, CC0) is photographed as a closed bound volume, so no halftone is visible. Not usable.

- (commons) halftone: no real halftone enlargements found (searched 'halftone dots macro', 'halftone screen enlarged', 'Rasterpunkte Druck'); make it in code.
- (commons) tins: lithographed tin containers on plain backgrounds are mostly CC BY-SA on Commons; only one PD object (a tin cigar-advert clock). The Van Gend & Loos cigar tin (141491949, CC0) has small drawn figures and was rejected; Red Indian cut plug tin rejected as a racist caricature.
- (commons) labels: only one clean candidate. Orange-crate labels from the Orange County Archives carry a hidden CC-BY-2.0 licence alongside the PD tag (ambiguous, so skipped); cigar labels found showed portraits.
- (commons) classifieds: no true want-ads page (dense small classifieds) on Commons; brought dense 1880-81 advertising pages instead. Chronicling America (LoC) is the better source.
- (commons) wood-type: no PD/CC0 photo of wood type blocks (only Unsplash stock photos, excluded); brought printed wood-type specimens instead.
- (commons) billboard: no frontal torn or layered paste-up poster wall under PD/CC0; brought a peeling Highsmith billboard and an oblique 1860 Marville street of posters.

Where blocked, the largest standard rendition (3840, 1920, 1280 or 960 px wide) was saved instead; each candidate's notes say which. image_url holds the original for a later re-fetch.

- (loc) poster-stamps: the only hit (2005684595, Arbeitsfront printing samples 1914) says "Rights status of individual images not evaluated", so it was skipped.
- (loc) tins: no photographs of lithographed tin containers found. The trademark deposits are paper labels; the can labels in [labels] could double.
- (loc) halftone: not searched in depth. LoC search does not surface macro/enlarged halftone scans; better made in code as the brief allows.

- (loc) television: only people, studios and 1929 experimental sets; no clean test pattern or CRT close-up.
- (loc) punch-cards: Hollerith and Census photos all show operators.
