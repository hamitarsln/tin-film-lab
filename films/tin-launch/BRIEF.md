# Brief: Tin launch film

## The film in one line
A coding agent already ships the product; plug in Tin and the same agent starts running the marketing,
as real workflows that keep going after the session ends.

## Who it's for
Technical founders and small teams who ship every day with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor and have no
marketing person. Their product moves fast. Their marketing is a blank page, or a pile of one-off prompts
that forget everything by the next session.

The viewer should leave knowing three things: what Tin is, that their own agent drives it, and the one
command to try it.

## What makes Tin different (use these, in your words, not all of them)
1. **It plugs into the agent you already use.** One MCP command in Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. Your agent
   reads your repository and fills Tin's onboarding plan from the codebase, so you don't fill in forms.
2. **A whole marketing system, not a bunch of skills.** 60 workflows across organic search, content, social,
   outreach, product QA, paid ads and creative work.
3. **Work that continues between sessions.** Save a workflow with its inputs and a schedule. Tin handles the
   timers, retries and waits for your approval.
4. **Review before anything ships.** Read an article, request changes, approve a campaign, an X post or an ad
   launch. Approved pages reach your site as a pull request or a commit to main.
5. **Open source.**

## Look
Tin's own world, from `refs/brand/DESIGN.md`: warm paper (#F5F1E6, #FEFDFB), ink (#080808), Funktional
Grotesk for words (`refs/brand/fonts/`), monospace for chrome and commands, sharp corners, hairline rules.
Terminal blue (#90D7E4) belongs to the agent's surface only. Lime (oklch(0.90 0.22 120)) is the call to
action. Butter, blossom and green are status colours (decisions, down, up).

Named style to direct from: an AI-lab launch film crossed with an Apple keynote bumper. A light page, one
typeface, black ink, real UI driven by a cursor, captions typed word by word, the camera always drifting,
one continuous take where each scene grows out of the last instead of cutting. 2D only.

## Format
- 1920×1080, 60 fps, 24 to 34 seconds in whole bars of the chosen track.
- No voiceover. On-screen words carry the story, so keep them short and huge.
- End card: the Tin wordmark, the MCP command, tin.computer. Hold it while the music fades.

## Sound
Music from `music/library/` only. Optional sound effects (key clicks, UI ticks, a low hit on the drop) may be
downloaded from mixkit.co/free-sound-effects (free licence, no attribution) and placed on their measured
peaks. Never synthesize cheap beeps.
