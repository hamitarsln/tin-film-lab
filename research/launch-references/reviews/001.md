## Lessons

- Keep: Lift one meaningful UI row out of a dense table, then show its detail panel.
- Try: For Tin attribution, show two posts, ask which produced a lead, and open the matching tracked lead using three prepared UI states.
- Try: For Tin workflow proof, retain one campaign name from overview through run history to its output; use one accent color for that path.
- Avoid: Adding an agency workspace and payment before the main attribution example has a named, traceable result.
- Still open: Installation steps, real attribution accuracy, sound and exact transition paths remain unverified.
