## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same person through problem and brand introduction.
- Try: For a Tin small-business launch, film one owner before work and at a desk, then show a completed campaign in the same setting.
- Try: For a banking scenario, replace one lifestyle insert with two readable states: formation submitted and account ready; use real approved UI assets.
- Avoid: Leaving the practical service mostly to a brief website and URL.
- Still open: Whether viewers can name the formation offer, and how the unheard sound shapes the montage.
