## Lessons

- Keep: Keep category heading, logo row and price positions constant while changing examples.
- Try: For a Tin integrations film, use the same three-column layout for email, CRM and analytics with approved icons and one useful task per row.
- Try: For an API pricing film, retain the blue brand transition but replace the third category with a readable request and returned lead record.
- Avoid: Presenting small price figures too briefly to understand their units.
- Still open: Exact star transition mechanism and all audible sound choices remain unverified.
