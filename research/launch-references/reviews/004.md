## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat the same situation and change only the action needed to finish it.
- Try: For Tin, film an evening campaign issue twice: first laptop repair, then phone approval and return to rest; use identical room angles.
- Try: Show one Tin trigger form beside the person, then enlarge the resulting approval card enough to read on a phone.
- Avoid: Do not let the human relief scene replace readable evidence of the completed action.
- Still open: Exact message content, continuous transitions and all sound layers remain open.
