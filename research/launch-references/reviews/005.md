## Lessons

- Keep: Alternate the artwork in its real setting with a clean isolated view of the same asset.
- Try: For a Tin campaign case, pair each posted creative with its real street or feed placement, using the same crop to connect the two.
- Try: Test a hand-lettered campaign title that breaks apart as a foreground object crosses; use 4 fps proof frames before judging its path.
- Avoid: Avoid assuming viewers know the sponsor when the film never states its product function.
- Still open: Sound, artwork provenance and the software audience remain unverified.
