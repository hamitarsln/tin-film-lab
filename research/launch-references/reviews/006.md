## Lessons

- Keep: Show permission approval followed by a visible valid state.
- Try: For Tin email setup, capture one provider authorization and a clear Connected badge in a 15-second sequence.
- Try: Test a 5-second problem opening before the same demo, using one presenter shot and one concise friction label.
- Avoid: Letting the presenter opening delay the first actionable screen.
- Still open: Whether the tiny records remain readable on mobile; all sound choices remain open.
