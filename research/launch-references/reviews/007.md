## Lessons

- Keep: Show wrong logo and wrong font as specific corrections beside the failed designs.
- Try: For Tin brand onboarding, show one campaign grid before and after adding the brand kit; retain the exact brief and six slots.
- Try: For a Tin opening, use a two-second wrong-logo correction followed immediately by the brand-context action, using actual prepared UI states.
- Avoid: A long visual montage that delays the user task and a solution reveal that abandons that task.
- Still open: Actual shared-context behavior, audio, and most sub-second opening cuts are unverified.
