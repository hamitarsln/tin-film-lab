## Lessons

- Keep: Carry a simple color distinction from metaphor into product UI.
- Try: For Tin campaign comparison, introduce two colored cards, then reuse those colors in real A/B results.
- Try: For a trading launch, test a 3-second battle opening followed immediately by the actual buy slider and completion state.
- Avoid: Allowing a game-like opening to hide the software category for half the runtime.
- Still open: Exact sub-quarter-second battle-to-chart edit, all sound and mobile reading remain unverified.
