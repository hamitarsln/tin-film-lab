## Lessons

- Keep: Explain memory through a decision that would otherwise repeat an old mistake.
- Try: For Tin, show a standard campaign draft blocked by a remembered client constraint; pair the dialogue with the source note.
- Try: Test a short three-person comparison ending in one concrete approval-history recall, with the product name visible before the final card.
- Avoid: Avoid withholding both the brand and usable product proof until the end.
- Still open: Sound delivery, permissions and retrieval accuracy are not established.
