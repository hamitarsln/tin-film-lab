## Lessons

- Keep: Keep a stable image composition while highlighting one visible edit at a time.
- Try: For Tin creative revisions, use the same ad image for headline, product and crop changes; label each selected target with a colored pointer.
- Try: Compare a result with its previous version using a circular detail crop on the changed region, then return to the full ad.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting animated results or a promotional chart as independent speed or accuracy proof.
- Still open: Exact transitions, real latency, preservation of untouched pixels and sound remain open.
