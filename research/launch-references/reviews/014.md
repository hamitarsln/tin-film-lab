## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one request from dense text into a visible set of choices and controls.
- Try: For Tin, show a campaign-plan paragraph turning into channel cards with a budget slider; keep all option names consistent.
- Try: Use a four-box diagram to explain user inputs, agent choice, approved components and rendered result, followed immediately by the matching UI.
- Avoid: Avoid spending most of the opening on history before giving a new viewer a concrete product result.
- Still open: Exact interaction behavior, integration rules, claimed adoption and sound remain unverified.
