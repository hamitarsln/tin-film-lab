## Lessons

- Keep: Use a physical box to make lost knowledge visible.
- Try: For Tin team handover, show a departing owner’s brief, then the exact saved campaign steps retained in the product.
- Try: Replace the chess section with one privacy-request screen recording, a saved three-step playbook and a second completed request.
- Avoid: Stacking several metaphors before one real business task.
- Still open: How observed actions become verified playbooks, and all music/voice/effect choices.
