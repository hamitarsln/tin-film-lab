## Lessons

- Keep: Move from a close view of a changeable part to the whole system using it.
- Try: For a Tin integration demo, show one connector credential panel, the resulting workflow node and the finished campaign action in that order.
- Try: For a hardware film, keep a fixed camera on a module swap and place only its name and purpose beside the hands.
- Avoid: Avoid treating promotional movement samples as measured safety or latency proof.
- Still open: Actual motion continuity, audio, performance limits and component availability remain unverified.
