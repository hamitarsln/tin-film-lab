## Lessons

- Keep: Use one recurring connection motif to bridge historical footage and abstract diagrams.
- Try: For Tin, begin with a real approval handoff map and simplify it into three workflow nodes, then show the sent result.
- Try: Test a brief archival-style montage followed by one clean diagram, with a visible sentence naming the specific user problem.
- Avoid: Avoid ending an infrastructure metaphor without a concrete product action.
- Still open: Spoken delivery, music, exact transitions and the architecture claims remain unverified.
