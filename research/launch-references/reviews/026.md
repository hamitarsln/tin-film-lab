## Lessons

- Keep: Place a clear before/after emotional turn before showing task outputs.
- Try: For Tin, show an owner juggling three marketing tabs, then a calm fixed screen with one scheduled campaign and its result.
- Try: Test the lead-generation prompt and weekly schedule as a standalone 12-second cut with a still background and enlarged labels.
- Avoid: Making outside product jargon carry the category explanation.
- Still open: Whether prompt outputs are actual product behavior and how the unheard audio supports the physical story.
