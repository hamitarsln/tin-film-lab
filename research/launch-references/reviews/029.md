## Lessons

- Keep: Highlight one chart anomaly before expanding its explanation card.
- Try: For Tin, isolate one campaign spend spike, then enlarge its explanation and approval action on the same timeline.
- Try: Use a command-palette query to find one failed workflow; hold the resulting run and cause long enough to read.
- Avoid: Avoid letting decorative robot scenes replace readable investigation results.
- Still open: Exact transitions, flash intensity, search performance and all audio remain unverified.
