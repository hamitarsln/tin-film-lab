## Lessons

- Keep: Show scattered inputs resolving into a sourced briefing with the same material still visible.
- Try: For Tin, collect three campaign notes and a run result into cards, then show the daily briefing citing each card.
- Try: Use a recurring four-color marker to follow each input from capture through a combined campaign summary.
- Avoid: Avoid a collective-intelligence metaphor replacing the concrete capture and retrieval steps.
- Still open: Agent behavior, permissions, exact particle motion and sound remain unverified.
