## Lessons

- Keep: Use a small personal story to give a company mission a concrete origin.
- Try: For a Tin founder film, pair a short first-campaign memory with two hand-drawn objects, then show the modern workflow that solves it.
- Try: Test progressive sentence highlighting on a short mission statement, checking full readability on a phone before adding decorative illustration.
- Avoid: Avoid a long manifesto that never gives the viewer one product experience to try.
- Still open: Voice, music, exact text-to-speech sync, contrast at phone size and the future product remain unverified.
