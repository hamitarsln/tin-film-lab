## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same forest project from the real-world setting into the software states.
- Try: For Tin, open beside a real product, ask for its campaign, then show that same product in the generated asset and workflow output.
- Try: Show one source folder and one completed campaign preview with a single large cursor and a label naming the changed state.
- Avoid: Showing many creative tools without stating which action the assistant performed.
- Still open: Narration, audio design, actual integrations, number meaning and exact inter-frame motion remain unverified.
