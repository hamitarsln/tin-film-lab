## Lessons

- Keep: Show the request and a large result inside the same communication scene.
- Try: For Tin, put one campaign-creative request beside its returned image, with the result large enough to judge.
- Try: Test a retro-window launch offer with only the benefit, limit and CTA, avoiding competing decorative windows.
- Avoid: Avoid hiding offer conditions or task detail behind rapidly changing backgrounds and slogans.
- Still open: Audio, flash frequency, actual service behavior and free-offer conditions remain unverified.
