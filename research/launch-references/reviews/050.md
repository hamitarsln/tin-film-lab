## Lessons

- Keep: Follow the same person through start, reward, sharing and community.
- Try: For ReadnType, show one valid reading session becoming an ERS result, a share card and a peer challenge using real UI.
- Try: For a productivity film, test a shorter entry shot followed immediately by Start session, then retain the customized share-card payoff.
- Avoid: Using a long cinematic arrival before explaining the utility.
- Still open: Reward validity, activity detection, continuous effect motion and all audio choices are unverified.
