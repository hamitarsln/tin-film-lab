## Lessons

- Keep: Translate a participant-count claim into labeled groups around one shared screen.
- Try: For Tin agent coordination, place three role labels around one campaign and show how a review action changes the shared output.
- Try: For a technical announcement, pair a one-sentence definition with one annotated result and hold both before adding wider use cases.
- Avoid: Broad industry promises without a visible example and a long logo ending after the CTA.
- Still open: Actual model behavior, capacity, learning results, sound and exact motion remain unverified.
