## Lessons

- Keep: Reuse the exact selected sunset as the full-frame example, so a search visibly leads somewhere.
- Try: For Tin, search for one campaign asset, select it and show the same image in a finished post with its source visible.
- Try: For a creator story, interleave one personal reason with three relevant output examples and keep captions large enough for a separately tested phone view.
- Avoid: Letting the preservation story hide the last practical step of obtaining the asset.
- Still open: Usage rights, library size, dates, voice delivery, music and exact motion remain unverified.
