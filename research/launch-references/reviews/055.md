## Lessons

- Keep: Use one concrete physical action to explain an abstract data concern.
- Try: For Tin access controls, show a campaign draft being copied, then cut to the exact permission setting that controls sharing.
- Try: Test an opening on the phone photographing the board, followed by the owner reaction and product claim, to shorten time to the central issue.
- Avoid: Treating a privacy metaphor as evidence of actual privacy protection.
- Still open: Actual privacy behavior, scene production method, sound and audience interpretation remain unverified.
