## Lessons

- Keep: Use dated versions of one familiar page to make historical access concrete.
- Try: For Tin, show three dated campaign versions and select one to reveal its original creative and result.
- Try: Pair an archive-scale montage with one real query field and retrieved date so the visual metaphor leads to a usable action.
- Avoid: Avoid using a large archive count as a substitute for showing retrieval.
- Still open: Exact motion, historical authenticity, archive completeness and sound remain unverified.
