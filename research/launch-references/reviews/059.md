## Lessons

- Keep: Show the same graphic first as digital output and then in a practical application.
- Try: For Tin, generate three campaign illustration variants, select one and show it in the final ad layout.
- Try: Test a path-outline reveal followed by a close view of the same mark on packaging, keeping shape and color consistent.
- Avoid: Avoid treating attractive mockups as proof of valid editable vector output.
- Still open: Output validity, speed claims, material provenance and sound remain unverified.
