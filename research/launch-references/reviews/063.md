# 063 · Moe | Introducing Moe: the stock market for your stuff

**Product from the film:** Moe presents a way to see the market value of personal belongings and act on that value.

**Explains well:** A phone aimed at collectibles leads to a named card and visible price.

**Still unclear:** How prices are validated, how selling works and what No Listings means are not demonstrated.

## Lessons

- Keep: Show the physical collectible and its matching digital result together before isolating the result.
- Try: For Tin product capture, point a phone at one product, reveal its extracted card over the same shot, then use that card in a campaign.
- Try: Cut a 15-second version with scan, named result, source evidence and one explicit action, using four matched states.
- Avoid: Delaying the core scan for a long market-history montage or leaving conflicting listing language unexplained.
- Still open: Valuation accuracy, selling behavior, audio, exact transitions and source provenance remain unverified.
