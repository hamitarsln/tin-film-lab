## Lessons

- Keep: Turn a broad technology story into one explicit question the product must answer.
- Try: For Tin, ask whether an agent can update the correct campaign record, then show a test input, resulting state and check.
- Try: Compare one failed agent action with its corrected sandbox result using the same app screen and visible record ID.
- Avoid: Avoid ending a testing argument without a visible test case and result.
- Still open: Exact product scope, enterprise integrations, interview context, motion and audio remain unverified.
