## Lessons

- Keep: Follow a product beauty view with a close functional detail such as cargo loading or underwater foils.
- Try: For Tin workflow films, pair an overview screen with one enlarged input-to-output mechanism and a short label.
- Try: For a physical product launch, follow the same cargo box from loading to destination and reserve separate frames for verified specifications.
- Avoid: Letting attractive presentation imagery imply validated engineering or commercial performance.
- Still open: Prototype status, technical performance, asset provenance, sound and exact motion remain unverified.
