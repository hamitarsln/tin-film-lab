## Lessons

- Keep: Use a quiet name card to break a dense montage.
- Try: For Tin, test three quick problem shots followed by one held product name and a visible campaign result.
- Try: Build a 10-second teaser using cleared customer-work footage and one repeated visual motif, then add a plain task sentence.
- Avoid: Using mood and market footage as the only product explanation.
- Still open: Product function, clip provenance, sub-second edits and all sound are unverified.
