## Lessons

- Keep: Turn an abstract information-overload problem into a visible accumulation around one fixed subject.
- Try: For Tin, surround one campaign question with scattered notes, then reveal a small graph linking the correct source decision.
- Try: Follow a graph-building state with one agent answer and its source links, instead of another abstract montage.
- Avoid: Avoid using adoption metrics or graph visuals as proof of correctness.
- Still open: Graph behavior, metric authenticity, exact transitions and audio remain unverified.
