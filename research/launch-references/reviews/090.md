# 090 · Cero | Introducing Cero: Spending with Upside

**Product from the film:** Cero presents a spending app with rewards, prize-like benefits, a score and a credit line.

**Explains well:** Repeated checkout scenes connect the app to everyday spending, while enlarged phone panels show benefits.

**Still unclear:** Eligibility, probabilities, fees and the relationship between rewards, salary doubling and credit are not explained.

## Lessons

- Keep: Return to real-world use between feature graphics so the app stays tied to everyday action.
- Try: For Tin, alternate one real product shot with its campaign output and measurable result, retaining the same product throughout.
- Try: Use one simple gauge update after a completed workflow, with a clear label for what changed and why.
- Avoid: Stacking attractive benefit amounts without explaining their conditions or relationship.
- Still open: Financial terms, eligibility, outcome probabilities, sound and precise motion remain unverified.
