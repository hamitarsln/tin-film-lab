## Lessons

- Keep: Use related instrument imagery to establish one coherent technical theme.
- Try: For a Tin teaser, follow two technical context shots with a concrete campaign request and completed output.
- Try: Test the final card with a plain one-sentence product description beneath the name, using a larger text size than the creator credit.
- Avoid: Assuming technical mood and a company name explain a new product.
- Still open: Product purpose, exact motion, audio and source provenance remain unverified.
