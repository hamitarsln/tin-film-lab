## Lessons

- Keep: Carry a highlighted decision into the next action state.
- Try: For Tin campaign approval, start with a team chat choosing a version, then show that exact version selected and scheduled.
- Try: For a trading demo, keep one event from social post through venue comparison, amount and verified completion instead of switching examples.
- Avoid: Showing cheerful financial outcomes without a traceable completed transaction.
- Still open: Imagery authenticity, trade behavior, dense transitions and all audio remain unverified.
