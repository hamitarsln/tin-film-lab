## Lessons

- Keep: Use a full interface, one enlarged action, and one readable result.
- Try: Show a Tin campaign screen, zoom into approval, then show the confirmed destination and timestamp.
- Try: Replace an abstract integration graph with a single visible route from brief to approved campaign.
- Avoid: Avoid treating a verified label as evidence without a readable receipt.
- Still open: Trading performance, asset availability, exact motion and sound remain unverified.
