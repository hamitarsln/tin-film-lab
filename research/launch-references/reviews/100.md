## Lessons

- Keep: Make the agent explain gaps before asking to act.
- Try: For Tin, ask why a campaign is underperforming and display three evidence-linked causes before approval.
- Try: Show one completed outreach or campaign artifact, then a clearly dated performance change instead of an immediate unexplained percentage.
- Avoid: Avoid implying instant causal improvement with a rapidly rising score.
- Still open: Real action execution, score definition, timing, publication permissions and audio remain unverified.
