## Lessons

- Keep: Give each presenter one distinct job and one supporting artifact.
- Try: Show a Tin customer explaining one campaign problem, then cut directly to its brief, approval and published output.
- Try: Use a branded visual metaphor only after a complete product action, keeping the CTA and any offer terms readable.
- Avoid: Avoid using rising revenue overlays as proof of typical customer income.
- Still open: Presenter identities are taken from on-screen labels; earnings, offer eligibility, product execution, full-rate transitions and audio remain unverified.
