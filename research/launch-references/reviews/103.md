## Lessons

- Keep: Cut from the whole operator setup to the exact contact point where the task succeeds or fails.
- Try: For Tin correction learning, show one wrong draft, one human correction and the next matching draft with the corrected detail retained.
- Try: For a workflow demo, pair a wide process overview with a close result inset, and label the current stage rather than relying on narration.
- Avoid: Presenting a short successful action as proof of a stated benchmark or broad reliability.
- Still open: Benchmark validity, actual training procedure, force/speed detail, sound and continuous task execution remain unverified.
