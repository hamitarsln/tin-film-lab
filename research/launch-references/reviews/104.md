## Lessons

- Keep: Start with the user’s real context, then isolate one useful interface action.
- Try: Open a Tin film inside a busy small business, then show one campaign approval on a phone.
- Try: Pair the final positive reaction with a dated, readable campaign result rather than a generic green number.
- Avoid: Avoid equating celebratory footage with guaranteed product outcomes.
- Still open: Trade mechanics, financial outcomes, footage origin, exact motion and sound remain unverified.
