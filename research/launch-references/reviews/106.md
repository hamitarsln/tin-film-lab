## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same question format from the opener into actionable cards.
- Try: For Tin feedback, start with one customer question, show two choices, then reveal responses and a resulting campaign decision.
- Try: Test a single opinion-market walkthrough with entry, current position and explicit resolution rule before showing rankings.
- Avoid: Showing reward growth without explaining its cause.
- Still open: Market resolution, return claims, eye effect motion and all sound remain unverified.
