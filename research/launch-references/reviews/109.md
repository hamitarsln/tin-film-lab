## Lessons

- Keep: Return to the same clear phone screen between human reactions so the device remains connected to the story.
- Try: Add two short question-and-answer captions to the fact-check exchange and compare muted comprehension.
- Try: For Tin, film one user making a visible correction to a generated post, then show the saved result in the same setting.
- Avoid: Using smiling reactions as evidence of factual accuracy or low latency.
- Still open: Voice quality, overlap handling, transcript accuracy, translation accuracy and edited versus real response timing remain unknown.
