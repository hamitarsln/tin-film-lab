## Lessons

- Keep: Carry repeating gloves and masks across changing viewpoints to preserve scene identity.
- Try: Add a single real gameplay objective and completion state to the cash scene, then check if viewers can name the player action.
- Try: For Tin, carry one post from brief through draft to approval in a short three-step sequence.
- Avoid: Treating a cinematic action montage as proof of actual gameplay or platform features.
- Still open: Gameplay status, source engine, rules, availability, exact edits and all audio layers remain unknown.
