## Lessons

- Keep: Return to the opening human scene after the demonstrated workflow.
- Try: For a video assistant, show the same raw ten-second clip before upload and after the exported edit.
- Try: Pair one manual caption adjustment with one prompt revision, keeping the final caption result visible for three seconds.
- Avoid: Avoid using export completion as a substitute for output quality.
- Still open: How much of the shown edit came from the prompt, and how does the audio support the emotional change?
