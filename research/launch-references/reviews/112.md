## Lessons

- Keep: Use a clear prize card after showing the kind of work participants can make.
- Try: For a Tin campaign challenge, follow example campaign cards with one entry action and a deadline.
- Try: Build a two-second icon-to-wordmark sequence with six approved symbols, then hold the event name for two seconds.
- Avoid: Avoid closing an actionable challenge with only partner logos.
- Still open: What are the rules, and are the quick number changes readable at phone size?
