## Lessons

- Keep: Use repeated objects to make scale and selection understandable.
- Try: Show many incoming Tin opportunities becoming three evidence-backed campaign options.
- Try: Pair a customer-network scene with one named, permissioned outcome and a clear application or signup step.
- Avoid: Avoid unsourced scarcity or lifespan numbers as factual proof.
- Still open: Program statistics, eligibility, historical connections, exact transitions and audio delivery remain unverified.
