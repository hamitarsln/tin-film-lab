## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one named incident through evidence, code change and resolved state.
- Try: Begin at the positive team message and failure instead of the abstract technical montage, then check first-view understanding.
- Try: For Tin, show one rejected draft, highlight the exact issue, apply one visible correction and show the approved result.
- Avoid: Letting a resolved label imply proven autonomous repair or a guarantee that software never breaks.
- Still open: Actual approval and execution steps, accuracy of the shown fix, source of footage and audio design remain unverified.
