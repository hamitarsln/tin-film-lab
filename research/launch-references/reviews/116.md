## Lessons

- Keep: Show the user returning to the interrupted personal activity after resolving the problem.
- Try: For an incident assistant, show a dinner alert, one real root-cause panel and a healthy-service result before returning to dinner.
- Try: Compare the long face-overlay exchange with a 15-second version containing three captioned incident steps.
- Avoid: Do not rely on unreadable diagnostic graphics to explain the repair.
- Still open: What dialogue was actually said, and does the product support the depicted repair flow?
