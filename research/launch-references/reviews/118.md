## Lessons

- Keep: Use a short explicit requirements list to state who can take part.
- Try: Replace the banknote montage with one real brief, created asset and accepted delivery, then check whether viewers can describe the work.
- Try: For Tin, pair one campaign output with a measured outcome and clear date range before showing broader scale.
- Avoid: Using large revenue and view figures as a substitute for the product workflow or verified earnings.
- Still open: Creation tools, actual creator task, approval process, payment terms, metric basis and audio remain unverified.
