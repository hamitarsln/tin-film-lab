## Lessons

- Keep: Make the prompt and its resulting artifacts the center of the story.
- Try: Show one Tin business brief and three large outputs: audience plan, creative and approved schedule.
- Try: Use a short sketch-to-interface reveal, then spend the saved time on a confirmed publication state.
- Avoid: Avoid claiming an entire company is built when only small asset thumbnails are visible.
- Still open: Real business creation, output contents, exact motion, asset origins and audio delivery remain unverified.
