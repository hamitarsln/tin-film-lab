## Lessons

- Keep: Use concrete physical imagery to explain an abstract learning premise.
- Try: Hold one simulation example longer and show a visible input followed by its response, clearly labeling what is live.
- Try: For Tin, use one brief and one changed visual output to explain generation before listing other applications.
- Avoid: Treating a broad nature montage or real-time label as proof of accurate interactive world simulation.
- Still open: Simulation input, latency, physical consistency, availability, footage origins and audio remain unverified.
