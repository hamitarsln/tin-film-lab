## Lessons

- Keep: Reuse the opening footage inside the demonstrated editor to make the result easy to recognize.
- Try: Hold one eye reference beside its generated result for three seconds so viewers can inspect consistency.
- Try: For Tin, open with a completed campaign asset, then show that exact asset being made and saved from its brief.
- Avoid: Inferring execution speed or full automation from a compressed sequence of tool and editor states.
- Still open: Manual work, processing time, generation reliability, export quality and audio remain unverified.
