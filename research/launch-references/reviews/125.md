## Lessons

- Keep: Change from one central screen to a portrait mosaic to explain fragmented attention.
- Try: For expert discovery, turn a broadcast image into topic clusters and then reveal a real ranked result.
- Try: Test a shorter media-history opening followed by a query for one named topic and three useful profiles.
- Avoid: Avoid implying that a domain montage proves discovery quality.
- Still open: How are people ranked, and what audio texture supports the montage?
