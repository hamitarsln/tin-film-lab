## Lessons

- Keep: Show guidance on the exact screen where the learner is already working.
- Try: For a campaign builder, highlight one field at a time while the user creates a real first campaign.
- Try: Compare a full screen recording with the presenter version, keeping identical annotated steps and a completed result.
- Avoid: Avoid extending two examples into an unqualified universal capability claim.
- Still open: Was the final beat correct, and how reliably do the annotations match unfamiliar screens?
