## Lessons

- Keep: Use a repeated screen frame to unify visually different source clips.
- Try: For a trading demo, show a crowded terminal then the same task in three clearly labelled controls.
- Try: Keep the CRT opener for three seconds, then switch to a clean full-size UI until the result is readable.
- Avoid: Do not make speaker montage stand in for actual product proof.
- Still open: What functionality is live, and do the source clips support the implied positioning?
