## Lessons

- Keep: Pair a broad business-building idea with recognizable tools and real-world-looking team activity.
- Try: Replace several montage fragments with one shared project reaching its first completed transaction.
- Try: For Tin, show two teammates taking the same campaign from brief through approval to a scheduled post.
- Avoid: Assuming that viewers can infer a platform’s specific functionality from unrelated tool and growth fragments.
- Still open: Which tools are part of Whop, actual workflow, source rights, metric meaning and audio remain unverified.
