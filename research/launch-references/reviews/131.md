## Lessons

- Keep: Enlarge one shopper conversation before expanding to many similar interactions.
- Try: Replace one broad ROI claim with a single campaign period and clearly defined attributed-order result.
- Try: For Tin, show one response becoming an approved lead record, then reveal the larger queue.
- Avoid: Using animated growth figures as proof of measured commercial impact or autonomous execution.
- Still open: Metric basis, actual customer data, automation limits, consent flow, attribution and audio remain unverified.
