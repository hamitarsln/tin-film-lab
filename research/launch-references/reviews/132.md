## Lessons

- Keep: Make an abstract service concrete through one input, named criteria and an output.
- Try: Show a Tin ad evaluated against three brand rules, then show the exact corrected version.
- Try: Use one simple connector diagram for the agent service and follow it with a real approval record.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting subjective percentage scores without a clear basis.
- Still open: Funding, partnerships, quality scores, model improvement, exact animation and audio delivery remain unverified.
