## Lessons

- Keep: Place concise benefit labels directly over the imagery intended to illustrate them.
- Try: Replace a racing insert with a real order-to-fill sequence and a clearly defined timing comparison.
- Try: For Tin, follow one speed metaphor with the actual request and measured time to a saved draft.
- Avoid: Using borrowed-looking speed imagery as evidence of platform latency or trading quality.
- Still open: Execution performance, spreads, fees, mainnet availability, asset rights and audio remain unverified.
