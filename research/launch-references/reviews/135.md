## Lessons

- Keep: Link the technical promise to people watching in recognizable everyday settings.
- Try: Replace one setup location with a labeled original/localized dialogue comparison, checked through a validated audiovisual route.
- Try: For Tin, show the source and localized campaign clip before cutting to the audience-use story.
- Avoid: Treating smiling viewers or a symbolic glowing object as proof of lip-sync accuracy.
- Still open: Dialogue, language transitions, lip-sync quality, actual workflow, production readiness and all sound design remain unverified.
