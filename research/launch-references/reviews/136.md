## Lessons

- Keep: Let an opening process diagram reappear as real interface structure.
- Try: Map Tin research, draft, review, publish and report stages into one dashboard row.
- Try: Open one chart point to show a real campaign run, its approval and final output.
- Avoid: Avoid equating impressive aggregate metrics with a proven automation workflow.
- Still open: Metric correctness, automation setup, real execution, full-rate effects and sound remain unverified.
