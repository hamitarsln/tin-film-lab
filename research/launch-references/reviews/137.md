## Lessons

- Keep: Gather scattered content into one recognisable screen before revealing the programme.
- Try: For a Tin weekly digest, collect six campaign-news cards into one screen, then show the actual three-item summary.
- Try: Test an end card that holds the show name, one value sentence and schedule for four seconds.
- Avoid: Avoid topic variety replacing a concrete example of the show’s value.
- Still open: What is the programme format, and is the white transition comfortable at full speed?
