## Lessons

- Keep: Let a brief comic hook lead quickly to a concrete capability.
- Try: Interrupt a Tin founder intro with a completed campaign notification, then show the approved output.
- Try: Pair one customer quote with the exact campaign artifact and dated result it describes.
- Avoid: Avoid treating staged urgency and enthusiastic quotes as proof of business impact.
- Still open: Testimonial authenticity, network value, closed deals, offer terms and audio delivery remain unverified.
