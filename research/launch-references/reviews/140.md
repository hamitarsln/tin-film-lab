## Lessons

- Keep: Keep a consistent set around presenters while briefly giving key actions full-screen focus.
- Try: Use a phone request to begin a Tin campaign, then show three readable milestones and an actual live placement.
- Try: Replace growth-chart decoration with a dated campaign report and its underlying result.
- Avoid: Avoid equating a Publish button or storefront mockup with a verified running business.
- Still open: Price and offer are recorded only as film content; actual deployment, revenue, integrations, exact motion and audio remain unverified.
