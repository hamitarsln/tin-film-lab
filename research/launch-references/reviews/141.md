## Lessons

- Keep: Trace an automated check all the way to a precise, actionable finding.
- Try: Show Tin checking a campaign URL, catching a broken destination and marking the exact affected ad.
- Try: Pair a quality-improvement number with its sample size, comparison period and one inspectable example.
- Avoid: Avoid treating a verified label or internal percentage as independent proof.
- Still open: Actual bug reproduction, evaluation method, customer claims, beta availability and audio delivery remain unverified.
