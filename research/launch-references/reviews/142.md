## Lessons

- Keep: Show the difficulty before the achievement, using real human reactions.
- Try: Build a Tin customer story around one failed campaign, the specific correction and a dated outcome.
- Try: Use repeated framing to bookend a short company story, with clear dates on evidence clips.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting a selective archival montage as complete historical proof.
- Still open: Historical chronology, interview accuracy, source rights, disclaimer text and audio delivery remain unverified.
