## Lessons

- Keep: Carry a recommendation overlay from the trigger location to the real-world outcome.
- Try: For a meeting assistant, show arrival near an office, a meeting-prep card and the user opening the needed notes.
- Try: Compare the four-case montage with one coffee-route case that includes location consent, recommendation and choice.
- Avoid: Avoid treating a displayed boarding card as proof of autonomous check-in.
- Still open: How are location permissions, user intent and action confirmation handled?
