## Lessons

- Keep: Use ordinary real examples to make a simple workflow feel achievable.
- Try: Show a Tin user capturing three shop moments and receiving a draft post with editable notes.
- Try: Keep the final access instruction as a full sentence beside a clear URL.
- Avoid: Avoid promising zero editing without showing what the user can correct.
- Still open: Assembly rules, privacy, output quality, availability and audio delivery remain unverified.
