# 146 · Kosh | KOSH: USD Account Built for Asia

**Product from the film:** Kosh presents a global financial account for freelancers, creators and founders to receive, hold, spend and send money.

**Explains well:** The film links account opening to payment receipt, a card, spending, investment and transfer states.

**Still unclear:** Eligibility, fees, custody, yield conditions, investment risk and actual transfer performance are not established.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use a clear success state after each money action so the sequence has visible outcomes.
- Try: Shorten the founder setup and trace one client payment through receipt to a work-tool purchase, stating fees and elapsed time.
- Try: For Tin, follow one freelancer brief through creation, approval and publishing with a visible saved state after each step.
- Avoid: Letting rising balance animation imply assured returns or universal account availability.
- Still open: Eligibility, fees, custody, yield and reward terms, transfer speed, transcript discrepancy and audio remain unverified.
