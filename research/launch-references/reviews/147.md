## Lessons

- Keep: Keep launch copy and the destination visually simple after a complex intro.
- Try: Use a short Tin brand motif, then a clear campaign action and persistent signup URL.
- Try: Replace abstract network growth with a small input-to-output diagram tied to a real use case.
- Avoid: Avoid treating historical papers or ecosystem logos as product validation.
- Still open: Liquidity mechanism, current availability, financial outcomes, asset origins and audio remain unverified.
