## Lessons

- Keep: Ground an abstract community promise in real people together.
- Try: Show two Tin users meeting around a campaign problem and the artifact they build together.
- Try: Put eligibility and a direct joining link beside the final invitation.
- Avoid: Avoid implying famous archival figures endorse a new community.
- Still open: Community identity, member outcomes, asset origins, exact motion and sound remain unverified.
