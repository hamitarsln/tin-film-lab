## Lessons

- Keep: Let different users name the jobs they need help with, then connect those jobs to relevant product views.
- Try: Replace two category flashes with one marketing request, generated output, edit and saved state.
- Try: For Tin, follow a founder quote about approval delays with that exact approval workflow and its completed result.
- Avoid: Treating testimony or a broad module tour as proof of autonomous work quality.
- Still open: User identities, claimed outcomes, agent reliability, live availability and audio remain unverified.
