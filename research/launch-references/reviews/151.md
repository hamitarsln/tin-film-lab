## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one news question and the same user through selection to a visible result.
- Try: Replace part of the rocket montage with entry cost, settlement condition and final outcome in one consistent unit.
- Try: For Tin, keep one campaign question visible from brief to published asset and its defined result.
- Avoid: Using rising rockets and excited reactions as proof of returns or accurate market routing.
- Still open: Eligibility, fees, routing, settlement, displayed-value meaning, footage origins and audio remain unverified.
