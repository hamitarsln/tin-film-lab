## Lessons

- Keep: Follow the financial interface with a person receiving and using the money in a familiar setting.
- Try: Match one sender row and recipient notification by name and amount, adding elapsed time only if actually measured.
- Try: For Tin, carry one campaign title through creator screen, reviewer notification and final publication.
- Avoid: Treating a success overlay or rising amount as verified settlement speed, yield or universal access.
- Still open: Fees, eligibility, conversion, settlement, amount-growth basis, location authenticity and audio remain unverified.
