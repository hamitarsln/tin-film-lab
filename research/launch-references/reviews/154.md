## Lessons

- Keep: Make the story object become the final invitation: scrolls turn into a readable application.
- Try: Place an application-day note on the opening suitcase or door and repeat its design on the final paper.
- Try: For Tin, carry one marked brief through a playful team handoff and resolve it into the same campaign title on a publish screen.
- Avoid: Leaving all offer detail until the end when the viewer needs to understand the invitation sooner.
- Still open: The program content, audience requirements, chase motivation, effects methods and all sound layers remain open.
