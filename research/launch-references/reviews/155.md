## Lessons

- Keep: Give the final result a clear visual form and connect it to the task.
- Try: Show a Tin campaign comparison with objective criteria and a selected winner, then its approval.
- Try: Open with the concrete product action sooner and reserve spectacle for a brief transition.
- Avoid: Avoid making financial outcomes look guaranteed through victory imagery.
- Still open: Contest mechanics, settlement, financial results, source rights and sound remain unverified.
