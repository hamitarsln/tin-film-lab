## Lessons

- Keep: Move from a simple privacy metaphor into concrete amount, receiver and completion states.
- Try: Compare the same transaction fields before and after shielding, with clear viewer labels.
- Try: For Tin, connect one campaign title, publish action and destination result in a short continuous demonstration.
- Avoid: Letting a broad privacy slogan stand in for an explanation of the information being hidden.
- Still open: The privacy boundary, actual protections, performance, footage methods and audio remain unverified.
