## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same specific question visible beside the matching diagram output.
- Try: Replace some office montage with a step-by-step Earth/black-hole clock comparison that teaches one point.
- Try: For Tin, retain the campaign brief beside its asset and highlight one shared requirement.
- Avoid: Spending more time saying explanations work than letting one explanation work for the viewer.
- Still open: Output teaching quality, scientific correctness, generation behavior, language range, asset origin and audio remain unverified.
