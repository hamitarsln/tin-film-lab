## Lessons

- Keep: Attach the product question directly to the scene it concerns.
- Try: For a live audience tool, show one yes/no prompt, one vote and the visible final result on the same clip.
- Try: Test a clean front-facing prediction card against the mirrored reaching-card scene for readability.
- Avoid: Avoid letting montage and funding replace a complete user action.
- Still open: How are predictions resolved, and which scenes are live versus replay examples?
