## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat essential phone text in a clean enlarged bubble beside the device.
- Try: For email triage, show the 30-email summary then a brief before/after inbox count with an undo control.
- Try: For follow-up drafting, show the casual instruction, generated draft and user approval before returning to travel footage.
- Avoid: Avoid implying a requested action has finished without a result state.
- Still open: How are automatic archiving and follow-up approval controlled?
