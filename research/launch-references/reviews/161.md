## Lessons

- Keep: Use one venue motif across opening, working scenes, rewards and CTA.
- Try: Replace a short underwater insert with a sample project card and readable entry requirement.
- Try: For Tin, introduce a closed campaign package and later reveal its finished assets after the creation steps.
- Avoid: Making entry instructions compete with a dense scenic montage.
- Still open: Selection rules, prize terms, actual event footage status, effects methods and audio remain unverified.
