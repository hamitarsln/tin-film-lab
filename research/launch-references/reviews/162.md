## Lessons

- Keep: Connect one real product event to a readable, channel-specific draft.
- Try: For Tin, show a shipped feature or new product record becoming LinkedIn and email drafts, with source links.
- Try: Follow voice setup with a before/after draft and a human approval, then show the actual published URL.
- Avoid: Avoid implying autonomous publication from a draft card or generic queue state.
- Still open: Source ingestion, brand fidelity, approval, publication, engagement and audio delivery remain unverified.
