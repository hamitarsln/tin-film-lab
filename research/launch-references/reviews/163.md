## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same card silhouette from opening suspense into the actual collection.
- Try: For a learning badge, use a two-second card turn then hold the earned achievement and requirement clearly.
- Try: Compare a clean one-card reveal with the multi-value montage, keeping rules and outcome visible for equal time.
- Avoid: Avoid giving value numbers more emphasis than the conditions needed to understand them.
- Still open: What are the pack rules and probabilities, and how does the reveal sound?
