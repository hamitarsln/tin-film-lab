## Lessons

- Keep: End a dense montage with a simpler repeated layout that names the intended outcomes.
- Try: Replace one cultural reference with a client brief, finished asset, distribution destination and scoped result.
- Try: For Tin, pair the actual campaign brief with its final post and channel preview.
- Avoid: Using famous brands and crowds as implied proof of the service being advertised.
- Still open: Concrete service scope, client examples, distribution process, measured retention, asset provenance and sound remain unverified.
