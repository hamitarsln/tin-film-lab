# 166 · Liquid | The edge is now yours

**Product from the film:** Liquid lets a user view a portfolio and request a trade suggestion through a chat interface, then confirm the order.

**Explains well:** The demo follows portfolio request, position request, analysis, suggestion and an explicit confirmation button.

**Still unclear:** The trade rationale and sizing details are dense and brief compared with the clear prompt and confirmation steps.

## Lessons

- Keep: Show analysis, a proposed action and a separate user confirmation in the same task chain.
- Try: Hold the proposal with readable position, size and reason before confirmation.
- Try: For Tin, show the proposed campaign change, approval summary and published result as separate visible steps.
- Avoid: Making the decision rationale harder to read than the action button.
- Still open: Rationale clarity, model/tool behavior, market accuracy, execution and audio remain unverified.
