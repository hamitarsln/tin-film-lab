## Lessons

- Keep: Build a repeated joke pattern, then turn it back onto the presenter for the final payoff.
- Try: Follow the parody disclaimer with one agent draft and one human review decision if product clarity is needed.
- Try: For Tin, show a marketer changing and approving an agent-proposed asset.
- Avoid: Treating satirical replacement or mock clinical claims as factual product evidence.
- Still open: Actual Agent A workflow, audience response to the joke, performance details and sound remain unverified.
