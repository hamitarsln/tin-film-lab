## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat one mascot across the fictional world, offer prop and final challenge.
- Try: Add a sample agent-entry card with required input and submission destination after the challenge.
- Try: For Tin, carry a campaign symbol through brief, asset and publication, ending with a readable action.
- Avoid: Letting spectacle and a scrolling ticker carry all of the participation instructions.
- Still open: Entry process, competition rules, reward terms, actual play, production method and audio remain unverified.
