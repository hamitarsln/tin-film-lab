## Lessons

- Keep: Pair the data collector with the exact view being collected.
- Try: For a field-research service, show the wearable device beside its recorded task view and a clear consent label.
- Try: Replace two logo variations with a booking step that names what is recorded and the participant benefit.
- Avoid: Avoid making the recording trade-off depend only on narration.
- Still open: What is recorded, how is it controlled, and what evidence supports the training benefit?
