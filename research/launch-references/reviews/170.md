# 170 · Clipping Hall of Fame | Welcome to the Future of Clipping

**Product from the film:** Clipping Hall of Fame presents training and access for people seeking a career making and distributing short clips.

**Explains well:** The course screens, clipping terminology and final Join Now card identify a beginner-to-clipper offer.

**Still unclear:** The film emphasizes reach and income but does not show one lesson producing a better clip or explain the joining requirements.

## Lessons

- Keep: Make the final enrollment action large and visually consistent with the course identity.
- Try: Demonstrate a raw clip, one lesson step and the revised clip before the CTA.
- Try: For Tin, compare the same campaign asset before and after one guided change against its brief.
- Avoid: Using aspirational earnings imagery as the only evidence of learning value.
- Still open: Lesson quality, joining requirements, typical outcomes, claim attribution, asset provenance and audio remain unverified.
