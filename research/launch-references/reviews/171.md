## Lessons

- Keep: Use close hands and tools to give an abstract builder audience a concrete visual identity.
- Try: For a procurement tool, show a technician needing a part then the actual order and receipt.
- Try: Test a shorter multi-industry montage followed by a five-second readable card-benefit screen.
- Avoid: Avoid leaving the whole practical offer to external post copy.
- Still open: Which financial benefits does a viewer understand without the source title or narration?
