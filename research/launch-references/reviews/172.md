## Lessons

- Keep: Show enough continuous task context for viewers to follow the work.
- Try: Show a Tin user approving a campaign, leaving the view, and a visible run completing its agreed steps.
- Try: Add a clear before/after artifact and completion count to a long automation demonstration.
- Avoid: Avoid calling repeated movement proof of autonomy or reliable task completion.
- Still open: Object identity, control mode, cycle success, safety, exact timing and audio remain unverified.
