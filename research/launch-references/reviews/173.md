## Lessons

- Keep: Use a physical photo wall to connect individual testimonials into one community.
- Try: For a builder cohort, show each participant portrait, one starting problem and one verified result in a consistent card.
- Try: Test a 60-second cut with three stories and a final five-second eligibility/application card.
- Avoid: Avoid relying on long uncaptioned testimonials for the essential offer.
- Still open: What are the actual programme terms, and which testimonial outcomes can be verified?
