## Lessons

- Keep: Place the discovered information beside the decision it changes.
- Try: For vendor research, show the supplier, one verified source record and a review decision in a single scenario.
- Try: Test a report card with a source link and match uncertainty against the claim-only overlay.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting staged allegations or broad discovery slogans as verified capability.
- Still open: How are identities matched, sources checked and incorrect findings corrected?
