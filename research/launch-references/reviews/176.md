## Lessons

- Keep: Keep one physical service metaphor while adding clearly separated technical chapters.
- Try: For a task router, show a wrong delivered object, then a precise request and the correct object beside its receipt.
- Try: For a payment tool, pair two physical tokens with a real source amount, fee and confirmed destination amount.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting equal amounts or compliance labels as demonstrated guarantees.
- Still open: What integration steps and failure handling are hidden behind the solver diagram?
