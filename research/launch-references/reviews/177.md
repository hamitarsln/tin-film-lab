## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat one interface pattern across different subjects to teach the grouping mechanism.
- Try: For a planning tool, group three scenario choices into one review card before confirmation.
- Try: For a prediction explainer, show one completed example including the result and whether all legs were required.
- Avoid: Avoid making news montage and large P&L imply verified or typical returns.
- Still open: Are the source clips authentic, and how does settlement connect to the selected parlay?
