## Lessons

- Keep: Keep one product constant while showing related agent capabilities.
- Try: Use one Tin product brief to show two ad variants, a documented test and the chosen next action.
- Try: Map three audience segments to three readable creatives, then show where each was approved to run.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting rising charts and revenue forecasts as verified outcomes.
- Still open: Store execution, test validity, conversion gains, transactions, source rights and audio delivery remain unverified.
