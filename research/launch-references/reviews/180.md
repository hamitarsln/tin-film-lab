## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat the same request-to-limit-to-approval pattern across different purchase types.
- Try: For an agent purchase demo, keep the requested $22 limit visible beside the merchant checkout and final receipt.
- Try: Replace a repeated purchase example with an over-limit rejection, then show the user changing the limit.
- Avoid: Avoid equating a permission checkmark with a completed merchant purchase.
- Still open: How are merchant, expiry and maximum-spend rules enforced during actual checkout?
