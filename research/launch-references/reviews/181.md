# 181 · Juno | Juno: AI Health Assistant for Chronic Illness

**Product from the film:** Juno is presented as a health companion that uses personal data to support daily planning and prepare a doctor-visit summary.

**Explains well:** The same person moves from a difficult evening at home to a prepared appointment, with the companion helping in both settings.

**Still unclear:** The floating character and panels do not show how the real phone app collects data, changes tasks or produces the doctor summary.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use the same person and companion across two concrete situations to show continuity of support.
- Try: Show the moved task and a readable doctor-summary preview in the actual phone interface.
- Try: For Tin, reveal the precise brief or report the assistant prepares for the users meeting.
- Avoid: Letting a friendly character stand in for every practical product step.
- Still open: Data entry, actual app workflow, summary content, clinical claims, integrations and audio remain unverified.
