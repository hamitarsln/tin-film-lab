## Lessons

- Keep: Use a clear visual release from dense problem evidence to a simple final promise.
- Try: Show one public wallet view beside the actual information visible after a private transfer.
- Try: For Tin, follow one draft permission problem to an approved publishing state with one highlighted setting.
- Avoid: Avoid using threat headlines as proof of product protection.
- Still open: Privacy behavior, security claims, source rights, actual motion and all audio delivery remain unverified.
