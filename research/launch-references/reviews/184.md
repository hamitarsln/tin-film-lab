## Lessons

- Keep: Use a small recurring persona to anchor a visually dense launch narrative.
- Try: Track one named company from idea to research, launch page and measured result if available.
- Try: For Tin, use one campaign title across research, creative and distribution tasks and show their combined output.
- Avoid: Treating a large field of task cards as a clear end-to-end product demonstration.
- Still open: Single-company workflow, actual autonomy, business/funding claims, asset provenance and audio remain unverified.
