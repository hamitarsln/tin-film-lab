## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same named feature visible from planning through the resulting application preview.
- Try: For a referral-feature demo, show two design options, the selected option and its matching final panel together.
- Try: Build a preview transition from an isolated feature card to its full app context, holding each readable state.
- Avoid: Avoid treating large green test labels as evidence of a verified build.
- Still open: How closely does the generated feature match selected decisions, and what tests actually ran?
