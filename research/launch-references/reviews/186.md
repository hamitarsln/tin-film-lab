## Lessons

- Keep: Keep stable people and table framing while changing locations to refresh a spoken explanation.
- Try: Film a Tin founder at one table and overlay a readable brief, approval and result from the same campaign.
- Try: Replace the seven-tool logo cluster with one before/after task map showing which steps Tin actually handles.
- Avoid: Avoid claiming that continuous activity removes the need for human growth judgment.
- Still open: Experiment quality, workspace access, approvals, growth results, exact overlay motion and audio delivery remain unverified.
