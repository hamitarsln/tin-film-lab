## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same output through agent work, review link and opened preview.
- Try: Show one Tin creative being generated, linked in a review message and opened in its final placement.
- Try: Add one readable test or approval result before the preview link to separate completion from verification.
- Avoid: Avoid using rapid build edits as evidence of actual build speed.
- Still open: Build timing, test results, integrations, adoption metrics, exact motion, asset rights and audio delivery remain unverified.
