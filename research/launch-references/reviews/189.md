## Lessons

- Keep: Move from a specific hidden fault to the wider system that must manage it.
- Try: Show one Tin campaign issue inside a creative, then its campaign card and portfolio alert with the same ID.
- Try: Add a short real detection timeline with alert, assigned owner and confirmed fix before the broad portfolio shot.
- Avoid: Avoid making illustrative location overlays stand in for measured detection and response.
- Still open: Sensor method, detection accuracy, actual deployment, maintenance response, cost claim, image provenance and audio delivery remain unverified.
