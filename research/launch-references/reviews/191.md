# 191 · Voquill | Voquill: Voice AI for Pathologists to Draft Reports in Real Time

**Product from the film:** Voquill presents an AI coworker that turns a pathologists spoken case observations into structured report sections.

**Explains well:** The opening shows reporting friction, then a spoken-observation graphic connects through LIS to a structured specimen report.

**Still unclear:** The report appears as an overlay, so the film does not show a complete case being reviewed and signed out in the actual lab system.

## Lessons

- Keep: Show the specific administrative friction before explaining the automation flow.
- Try: Track one observation into its report section and show explicit clinician review before sign-out.
- Try: For Tin, highlight one brief requirement as it enters the matching output section for review.
- Avoid: Letting a schematic report stand in for a complete reviewed case workflow.
- Still open: Actual system interaction, review behavior, clinical accuracy, integration coverage, statistic source and audio remain unverified.
