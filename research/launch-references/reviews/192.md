## Lessons

- Keep: Define one visual unit before expanding it into a complex system.
- Try: Show one Tin conversion event becoming a campaign group, then compare groups using a visible legend.
- Try: End a network explanation by selecting one flagged group and showing the evidence and next action.
- Avoid: Avoid leaving the final analytic map as an attractive but unactionable cloud.
- Still open: Identity accuracy, false positives, data provenance, investigator workflow, exact particle motion and all audio delivery remain unverified.
