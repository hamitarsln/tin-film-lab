## Lessons

- Keep: Pair first-person control views with an external view of the human and robot.
- Try: Show synchronized controller/robot action with measured timestamps and actual network context.
- Try: For Tin, pair approval and resulting campaign-state change with a shared asset identifier and measured timing.
- Avoid: Using sparse task footage to imply measured smoothness, latency or universal compatibility.
- Still open: Remote setup, measured response, compatibility, collision behavior, industrial-use claims and audio remain unverified.
