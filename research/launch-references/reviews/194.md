## Lessons

- Keep: Expand one playable card into a grid to communicate variety and discovery.
- Try: For a mini-game builder, keep one named prompt and show its generated game followed by a friend joining.
- Try: Start a short cut with the dart game, then expand to the grid and end on a readable creation prompt.
- Avoid: Avoid letting a long cultural montage replace the central creation demonstration.
- Still open: What changes between entering a prompt and playing the finished game?
