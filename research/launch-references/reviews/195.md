## Lessons

- Keep: Use the same practical task before and after the product reveal to explain the offer.
- Try: Show the supported package, human handoff and included monthly service beside the robot task.
- Try: For Tin, tie a defined included workflow to its finished asset and required user handoff.
- Avoid: Letting a starting price and progress overlay imply the full operating scope or autonomous reliability.
- Still open: Setup, supervision, supported tasks, total service scope, learning/throughput claims and audio remain unverified.
