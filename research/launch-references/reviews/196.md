**Product from the film:** Armature monitors and tests how AI agents use a product, then gives alerts, comparisons and improvement recommendations.

## Lessons

- Keep: Name one concrete tool-selection error instead of describing agent failure only in general terms.
- Try: Apply a tool-description recommendation and rerun the original lead-lookup task with the selected tool visible.
- Try: For Tin, connect one failed tool call to a corrected input and the same workflow succeeding after review.
- Avoid: Stopping the diagnostic story at recommendations when a short retest could show their value.
- Still open: Fix/retest behavior, actual coverage, benchmark basis, recommendation quality, setup time and audio remain unverified.
