## Lessons

- Keep: Tie each proposed creative to one visible source observation.
- Try: For a clothing campaign, show a fabric-review quote beside the ad headline derived from it and a human approval.
- Try: Build a three-column evidence, decision and creative view before expanding to the broader campaign canvas.
- Avoid: Avoid letting rising sales graphics stand in for a result from the demonstrated campaign.
- Still open: How does the approved creative reach a campaign, and how do results change the next plan?
