## Lessons

- Keep: Use a precise everyday question to reveal where company context gets lost.
- Try: For shared memory, repeat the PR-priority question in the product and show a sourced answer without interrupting the founder.
- Try: For customer context, show the original Codex note, extracted memory and corrected email draft together.
- Avoid: Avoid ending a clear problem skit with only a product metaphor.
- Still open: Where does a teammate ask a question, and how is the answer connected to its source?
