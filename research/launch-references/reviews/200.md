## Lessons

- Keep: Connect a gathering to the creative identity of its location using concrete local scenes.
- Try: Create a 90 s village invitation that names the offer first, then uses one music, invention and technology example before a participant-day view.
- Try: Use the changing-letterform city reveal for a local maker event, then hold a plain card with dates, cost and who should apply.
- Avoid: Avoid making viewers wait six minutes to learn the invitation and intended participant.
- Still open: What will participants build, where will they stay, and what are the dates and application requirements?
