# 201 · Index | Launching Index: Platform for AI Agents to Compensate Content Owners

**Product from the film:** Index by Parallel proposes a model where content owners can see the value of their content to AI agents and get paid.

**Explains well:** The change from browsing the open web to receiving answers from agents is expressed through a consistent network of dots.

**Still unclear:** The film does not show how a content owner joins, how content value is measured, or how payment happens.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use one recurring visual token to connect the problem and proposed solution.
- Try: Show one content-owner use and payment example after introducing the model.
- Try: For Tin, carry a brief card through generation, review and the real output.
- Avoid: Making the economic argument so long that the actual user benefit arrives only near the end.
- Still open: Joining, valuation method, payment terms, actual scale, exact motion and audio remain unresolved.
