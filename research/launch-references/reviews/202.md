## Lessons

- Keep: Make the old workflow tangible with specific physical tasks before simplifying the input.
- Try: Show a Tin brief copied across tools, then one message producing a readable campaign plan with the same requirements.
- Try: Keep a part specification highlighted from request through two quotes and the selected order.
- Avoid: Avoid giving savings claims more screen space than the output that explains them.
- Still open: Quote quality, savings baseline, order completion, source provenance, exact transitions and audio delivery remain unverified.
