# 203 · Archer | Introducing Archer: global trading engine for every asset, fully onchain on Solana

**Product from the film:** Archer is presented as one trading platform, with wallet connection and a market chart interface.

**Explains well:** The montage connects major market news, different assets and continuous trading to one platform.

**Still unclear:** The short interface view does not explain which instrument is traded, how an order is placed, or what the user receives.

## Lessons

- Keep: Isolate one entry action after a dense context montage.
- Try: Show one named instrument and a complete order result.
- Try: For Tin, shorten context and show the brief, output and approval action.
- Avoid: Giving the product interface less time than the surrounding market imagery.
- Still open: Instrument meaning, actual asset coverage, order execution, clip origin and audio remain unresolved.
