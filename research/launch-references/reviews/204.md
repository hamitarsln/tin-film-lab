## Lessons

- Keep: Use a common center to explain how related specialist tasks share information.
- Try: Show a Tin test result entering a campaign memory, then visibly changing the next creative brief.
- Try: Replace one abstract particle shot with a before/after recommendation tied to the same input data.
- Avoid: Avoid letting a broad capability map replace a concrete example of learning.
- Still open: Scientific validity, research outcomes, time savings, diagram data, source rights and all audio delivery remain unverified.
