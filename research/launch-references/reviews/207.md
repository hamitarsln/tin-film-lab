## Lessons

- Keep: Show the physical parts that correspond to each step in the mechanism.
- Try: For a Tin automation, show input data, decision rule and output side by side with one real run.
- Try: For the headband story, replace one brain animation with a recorded signal and clearly labeled stimulation marker.
- Avoid: Avoid expanding from a prototype explanation into broad health benefits without a shown evidence path.
- Still open: Device performance, research applicability, physiological benefits, safety, exact stimulus timing, cognitive claims and all audio delivery remain unverified.
