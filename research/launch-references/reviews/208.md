## Lessons

- Keep: State the access problem in a short, readable comparison.
- Try: Turn the device to show a named instrument and order/result state.
- Try: For Tin, put the actual campaign output inside the reveal.
- Avoid: Using an attractive device exterior as the only product demonstration.
- Still open: Instrument, eligibility, access method, QR destination, claim context and audio remain unresolved.
