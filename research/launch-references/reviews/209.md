# 209 · Prism | Prism: AI-Native Recruiting Agency

**Product from the film:** Prism is an AI recruiting service that turns a role brief into selected candidates and interview conversations.

**Explains well:** The same recruiter moves from paper overload and poor interviews to a short brief, refined candidates and productive meetings.

**Still unclear:** The film does not show why one candidate meets the updated requirements or which outreach decisions the recruiter approves.

## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one urgent task through a physical problem, a brief software workflow and a human payoff.
- Try: Show the updated B2B requirement beside one candidate fact that explains why the shortlist changed.
- Try: Insert a short outreach approval and response step before the first interview handshake.
- Avoid: Avoid letting attractive candidate cards replace the explanation of fit.
- Still open: How are role requirements checked against evidence, and what does the recruiter approve before candidates are contacted?
