## Lessons

- Keep: Use one product and a clear input prompt to connect a wide range of generation steps.
- Try: Replace one verified montage with a visible failed product detail, correction and user approval.
- Try: Show two finished variants side by side with the exact platform-specific changes labelled.
- Avoid: Avoid using glowing verification labels as the whole explanation of quality control.
- Still open: What is checked, what gets corrected, and where does the user approve the final ad?
