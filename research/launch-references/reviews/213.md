## Lessons

- Keep: Preserve the input scene layout when revealing its structured interpretation.
- Try: For Tin, turn one ad screenshot into labeled creative fields without changing its layout.
- Try: Follow a highlighted detected issue into the actual task or approval record it creates.
- Avoid: Avoid ending at an attractive analytic overlay without showing how people use its output.
- Still open: Measurement accuracy, live model behavior, damage classification, output workflow, flash intensity and all audio delivery remain unverified.
