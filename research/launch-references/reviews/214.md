# 214 · Riffle | Introducing Riffle: the modern playground for music creation

**Product from the film:** Riffle presents a collaborative music workspace with samples, layered tracks and an assistant for musical parts.

**Explains well:** A selected sample becomes a track, an assistant proposes a bassline, and the workspace leads to a shareable player.

**Still unclear:** The rapid collage makes it hard to tell which musical changes belong to the same piece, especially without hearing the audio.

## Lessons

- Keep: Make AI assistance a visible addition to a human-owned artifact.
- Try: Show a Tin user making a campaign, asking for one missing asset and adding the result into the existing plan.
- Try: Preserve one creation from selection through edit and shared preview with matching labels at each step.
- Avoid: Avoid using dense collage to hide the actual difference made by a feature.
- Still open: Audio changes, musical quality, precise transitions, collaboration behavior, asset rights and all soundtrack details remain unverified.
