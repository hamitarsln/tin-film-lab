## Lessons

- Keep: Use a simple conceptual contrast before introducing a complex mechanism.
- Try: Animate three private bids and show exactly how supply produces one clearing price and allocations.
- Try: Label the relationship between Crafts and Raise Zero when the auction screen first appears, then show the first user action.
- Avoid: Avoid letting historical prestige and moving numbers substitute for a mechanism explanation.
- Still open: How do bids and supply produce the price, and what is the relationship between the two displayed names?
