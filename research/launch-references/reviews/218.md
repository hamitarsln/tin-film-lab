# 218 · Mark | Mark raises $1M for Mark II $159 AI bookmark

**Product from the film:** Mark II combines a bookmark and a small scanner/voice recorder with a phone library for reading quotes and ideas.

**Explains well:** Hands using the two physical parts explain how the device belongs with a book, while the app shows quotes linked to books.

**Still unclear:** The film never clearly follows the same scanned passage and spoken thought into their saved app record, leaving the capture-to-library link implicit.

## Lessons

- Keep: Explain a physical product through hands using it in the setting where it belongs.
- Try: Track one printed sentence into the app with the same words, book source and attached spoken thought.
- Try: Shorten the repeated philosophy section and use that time to show offline capture syncing when the phone returns.
- Avoid: Avoid relying on separate capture and library shots to imply a complete workflow.
- Still open: How does the user know a capture succeeded, and how are the exact passage, book and thought joined?
