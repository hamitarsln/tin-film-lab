## Lessons

- Keep: Use a clear palette change to mark the shift from problem to alternative.
- Try: Open a Tin film with one brief parody of manual campaign work, then show the full corrected workflow.
- Try: Use the same campaign card in muted and bright states to make the actual before/after change visible.
- Avoid: Avoid spending nearly the whole film on tone while leaving the main interaction unexplained.
- Still open: Grid mechanics, trading results, precise transitions, flash intensity, source rights and all sound remain unverified.
