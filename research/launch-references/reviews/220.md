## Lessons

- Keep: Use a stable input surface to connect different users and contexts.
- Try: Show one Tin request in a real work setting, then its returned plan before switching users.
- Try: Replace translucent text over footage with a brief solid-background close-up at the important input and answer.
- Avoid: Avoid a request-only montage that leaves the value of the answer unseen.
- Still open: Response content, route selection, wallet analysis, execution, exact typing motion and audio delivery remain unverified.
