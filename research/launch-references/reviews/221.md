## Lessons

- Keep: Connect several app actions through one meeting with a clear before-and-after sequence.
- Try: Show the meeting notes beside the generated summary email, including a visible review and send step.
- Try: Show the ATS record before and after the interview workflow, with only the changed fields highlighted.
- Avoid: Avoid making completion badges carry all the proof of a finished workflow.
- Still open: What exact records changed, and where can the user review them before actions are taken?
