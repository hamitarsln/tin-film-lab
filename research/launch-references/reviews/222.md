## Lessons

- Keep: Use short chapter statements to guide a fast montage.
- Try: Show a Tin post moving from discovery to a documented creative decision, keeping the same thumbnail visible.
- Try: Replace one abstract growth figure with a readable input, action and result card from a single case.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting green gains without explaining the underlying action.
- Still open: Content-position mechanics, returns, payment support, source rights, exact motion and all audio delivery remain unverified.
