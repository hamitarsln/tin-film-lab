## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat a clear visual word across different materials to hold a montage together.
- Try: Add one accurately labeled privacy use case with action and result.
- Try: For Tin, connect the brand statement to a real brief, asset and next step.
- Avoid: Letting a strong theme replace a practical explanation of the product.
- Still open: Product mechanism, intended user, integration, clip provenance, final-language meaning and audio remain unresolved.
