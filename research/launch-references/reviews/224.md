## Lessons

- Keep: Use two task scales to explain why a more advanced execution tool matters.
- Try: Show a small Tin publish action beside a larger batch workflow with visible progress and approvals.
- Try: Keep the settled input, output and savings basis readable together after any animated count.
- Avoid: Avoid cutting away immediately after a numerical count; sparse samples can catch an intermediate value.
- Still open: Execution, price comparison, savings math, Chinese script accuracy, footage rights, exact transitions and all audio remain unverified.
