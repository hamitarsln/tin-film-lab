# 225 · Hearing Aid Startup | Reinvented hearing aid: $20 vs $4700

**Product from the film:** The team presents a custom mechanical hearing-support device shaped from ear images and a hearing profile.

**Explains well:** Ear anatomy is placed beside the proposed membrane-and-lever mechanism, then physical prototypes and fitting software make the build process tangible.

**Still unclear:** The custom profile is not followed into a final fitted device or a measured hearing result; the final explanation also cuts off before that handoff is clear.

## Lessons

- Keep: Place the proposed mechanism beside the familiar system it imitates.
- Try: Show a Tin brand profile beside the creative choices it controls, with matching labels.
- Try: Follow one input profile through generation, fabrication or publishing and a defined result instead of ending on process footage.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting an anatomical analogy as proof of clinical effectiveness.
- Still open: Hearing benefit, suitability, fitting precision, price comparison, production cost, research basis, source rights and all audio delivery remain unverified.
