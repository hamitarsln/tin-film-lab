## Lessons

- Keep: Use a consistent theme across varied footage to establish a strong brand attitude.
- Try: Expand the account sequence to show the input company, populated fields and why a suggested contact matters.
- Try: Keep three strong competition clips and use the recovered time for one clear sales outcome.
- Avoid: Avoid giving product explanation only a two-second slot inside a long montage.
- Still open: What does Start initiate, and what useful sales action follows the suggested contacts?
