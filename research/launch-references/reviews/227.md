# 227 · Ponder | Ponder: agentic video editor raises $2.5M pre-seed

**Product from the film:** Ponder is a video-editing partner that reviews footage, proposes an edit plan, asks for preferences and assembles a timeline.

**Explains well:** The film makes human choice visible through a take-selection question and shows planning, footage review and timeline assembly.

**Still unclear:** Many short interface and testimonial cuts prevent the viewer from following one chosen answer through to its exact change in the finished edit.

## Lessons

- Keep: Show the assistant asking a meaningful creative preference question.
- Try: Trace one answered question through timeline placement and playback.
- Try: For Tin, show a tone choice changing the same asset before approval.
- Avoid: Using many testimonial and interface fragments where one complete decision loop would be clearer.
- Still open: Measured time savings, edit quality, actual use in this films production, production software and audio remain unverified.
