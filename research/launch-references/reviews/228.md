## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the subject stable when demonstrating visual variation.
- Try: Use one Tin product image to compare three explicit style settings with the control visible.
- Try: Show one preference action, its saved state and the next output that reflects it.
- Avoid: Avoid using a changing gallery as the only explanation of preference learning.
- Still open: Generation provenance, learning behavior, parameter effects, exact motion, image rights and all audio delivery remain unverified.
