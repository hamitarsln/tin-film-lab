## Lessons

- Keep: Use a repeated participation theme to connect varied cultural images.
- Try: Pair a Tin audience story with one campaign that the audience can now create or run.
- Try: Replace one achievement montage with a clear before/after access screen and a completed first action.
- Avoid: Avoid expecting cultural pride to explain an unfamiliar financial product.
- Still open: Specific product scope, market access, availability, source rights, historical framing and all audio delivery remain unverified.
