## Lessons

- Keep: Use one practical project to connect several introductions into a memorable story.
- Try: Show the original filmmaker request beside the notification and the matched person accepting it.
- Try: Add a brief text cue near the first referral so silent viewers understand the launch-film task before the late reveal.
- Avoid: Avoid leaving the viewer with only a notification and no way to understand how a match begins.
- Still open: What does a user ask for, why is the person suitable, and how do both sides accept the introduction?
