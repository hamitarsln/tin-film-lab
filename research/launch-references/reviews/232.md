# 232 · Tuyo | Buy Now, Pay Maybe

## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one merchant and amount across list, result and real setting.
- Try: Show a named Tin campaign from its list row to a published result, keeping its title visible.
- Try: Add a short rule beside a surprising reward so viewers understand what caused it.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting a chance-based reward without explaining its basic conditions.
- Still open: Eligibility, odds, payment setup, notification authenticity, footage origin and all audio remain unverified.
