# 236 · Astrocade | Astrocade Raises $56M to Let Anyone Create Games with AI

**Product from the film:** Astrocade is presented as a platform for playing, creating and sharing games.

**Explains well:** The founders turn their creation claim into a small story where one makes a game and the other becomes distracted playing it.

**Still unclear:** The game-making input and settings are too small to follow, so the film shows a creation claim and playable result without explaining the build step.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use a human handoff to make a generated result feel worth trying.
- Try: Show one person creating a Tin campaign and another opening its finished preview during a conversation.
- Try: Keep the creation prompt visible beside the result before returning to character reactions.
- Avoid: Avoid making easy creation depend on unreadable controls and a spoken claim.
- Still open: Exact creation input, time-to-result, counters, funding/reward claims, effects production and all audio remain unverified.
