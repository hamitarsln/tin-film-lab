# 237 · Interact AI | Introducing Interact AI: a new interface for the web

**Product from the film:** Interact AI puts a conversational agent on a website to answer questions, show product information, guide a visitor and support demo booking, with visitor analytics.

**Explains well:** The example progresses from a website greeting to product views, integrations, a booking form and the business-side visitor table.

**Still unclear:** The car analogy takes nearly half the film, and the visitor question is not displayed consistently enough to pair every generated view with its exact request.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the guide visually stable while relevant product views change.
- Try: Caption requests and link one visitor journey to the final booked-lead row.
- Try: For Tin, trace one request through its asset and approved or scheduled record.
- Avoid: Spending half the runtime on an analogy before demonstrating the actual product.
- Still open: Speaker origin, response accuracy, latency, booking completion, intent inference and audio remain unverified.
