## Lessons

- Keep: Pair a human concern with a task result while keeping the person visible in the setting.
- Try: Show a Tin user discussing a launch deadline while a named campaign moves from brief to approved draft.
- Try: Hold one readable request and result before pulling back to a multi-agent overview.
- Avoid: Avoid using a broad role map and completed screens as the full explanation of agent coordination.
- Still open: Exact task inputs, permission steps, output correctness, response latency, screen production and all audio remain unverified.
