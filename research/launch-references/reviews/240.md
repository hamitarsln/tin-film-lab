## Lessons

- Keep: Use a clear problem-to-feature sequence with consistent visual materials.
- Try: Show one Tin campaign through setup, approval and delivery before adding related tools.
- Try: Compare a flat readable form shot with the tilted version to see which details viewers retain.
- Avoid: Avoid making a feature carousel stand in for a completed task.
- Still open: Eligibility, supported jurisdictions, transfer completion, cashback terms, savings basis and all audio remain unverified.
