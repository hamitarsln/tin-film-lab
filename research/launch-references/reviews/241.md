## Lessons

- Keep: Mirror the manual workflow with the solution using the same stage order.
- Try: Show manual campaign briefing, drafting and publishing, then repeat those stages with Tin support.
- Try: Highlight a source phrase and its resulting output field, while keeping an approval step visible.
- Avoid: Avoid absolute outcome claims without showing exceptions and the human review point.
- Still open: Loss calculation, coding correctness, payer coverage, appeal results, staff oversight and all audio remain unverified.
