# 242 · Andco | Andco: AI agents for personal injury law firm document workflows

**Product from the film:** Andco presents agents that retrieve case documents for personal injury law firms.

**Explains well:** A car-accident example makes the need for police, medical and insurance records concrete before the product appears.

**Still unclear:** The film shows follow-up activity but does not clearly show how a lawyer starts a request or checks that the returned record set is complete.

## Lessons

- Keep: Start with a specific human situation that explains why the workflow matters.
- Try: Open a Tin film with a launch owner missing campaign assets, then show the exact collection request and delivered set.
- Try: Pair a customer speed claim with a visible before-and-after case timeline and a clear measurement scope.
- Avoid: Avoid letting testimonials replace a readable task start and completion check.
- Still open: Retrieval setup, record completeness, exception handling, customer metrics, volume claims and all audio remain unverified.
