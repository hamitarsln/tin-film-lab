## Lessons

- Keep: Connect each manual burden to the product screen that addresses it.
- Try: Show manual campaign asset collection beside Tin source connections and the resulting draft.
- Try: Keep one uncertain output visible and show the operator correcting it before completion.
- Avoid: Avoid implying complete automation from percentage claims without showing the remaining cases.
- Still open: Matching accuracy, customer metrics, time savings, exception handling, source permissions and all audio remain unverified.
