## Lessons

- Keep: Use familiar objects to explain why an unseen business process matters.
- Try: Continue the running-shirt example into a supplier comparison with price, delivery date and evidence for one compliance check.
- Try: Show the broker reviewing a software flag and asking a factory for the missing document before recommendation.
- Avoid: Avoid making green checks the entire explanation of supplier trust.
- Still open: What evidence supports the checks, and how does the broker choose among the identified suppliers?
