# 246 · ReasonBlocks | ReasonBlocks: AI runtime that catches agent failures mid-run and learns

**Product from the film:** ReasonBlocks is presented as an agent runtime that detects failure loops, reuses recovery patterns, shows diagnostics and compresses context.

**Explains well:** A side-by-side trace shows a repeating parse error versus an inserted loop guard followed by successful steps.

**Still unclear:** The integration step is missing, and No retries could mean no further retries after recovery; that scope is not stated beside the earlier two-retry example.

## Lessons

- Keep: Make the corrective step visible between failure and success.
- Try: Align retry wording and show the integration change for the example.
- Try: For Tin, show the failed step, exact correction and matching successful result.
- Avoid: Leaving the scope of a short recovery promise unclear beside a more specific example.
- Still open: Illustrative versus recorded traces, benchmark method, integration, recovery limits and audio remain unresolved.
