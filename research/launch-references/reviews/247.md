# 247 · Salesgraph | Salesgraph: 24/7 AI Marketers, Analysts & Strategists for Every Sales Deal

**Product from the film:** Salesgraph gathers sales context and prepares deal insights and collateral, delivered through a Slack interface to help representatives manage strategic deals.

**Explains well:** The script identifies context retrieval and collateral building, while the later message example shows discovery gaps and action items.

**Still unclear:** The film spends most of its time explaining the sales-support model but does not trace one source conversation into a recommendation, collateral asset or completed deal action.

## Lessons

- Keep: Map the roles and artifacts a product brings together.
- Try: Trace one call excerpt through a finding, asset and chosen action.
- Try: For Tin, show context and a resulting artifact before the wider agent-team explanation.
- Avoid: Delaying the first concrete product output until late in a long founder pitch.
- Still open: Source linkage, output quality, integration coverage, deal results and audio remain unverified.
