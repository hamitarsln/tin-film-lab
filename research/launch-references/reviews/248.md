## Lessons

- Keep: Show an automation benefit through the changed interactions of a real-looking team.
- Try: Add a short reveal of one experiment with the original page, variant and result before the CTA.
- Try: Show a rejected experiment alongside a winner to explain how Sherpa decides which changes to retain.
- Avoid: Avoid leaving the claimed improvement disconnected from a visible experiment.
- Still open: Which change produced the result, and how does the system decide whether to keep it?
