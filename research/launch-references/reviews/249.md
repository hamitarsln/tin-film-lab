## Lessons

- Keep: Keep named student examples beside their specific campus tools.
- Try: For a campus event app, show one prompt, generated event page, share action and friend opening the same page.
- Try: Replace the 70–108 s multi-screen collage with three labelled steps using one rent map, keeping other examples as a brief closing grid.
- Avoid: Avoid changing the app before its creation and sharing path can be followed.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; adoption claims and actual generation/distribution remain unverified.
