# 250 · Epoch Protocol | EPOCH Mainnet is Live

**Product from the film:** EPOCH announces a live mainnet and positions itself around coordination across Web3 systems.

## Lessons

- Keep: Make launch status explicit in the final card.
- Try: Show one fragmented starting state, user request and coordinated result.
- Try: For Tin, connect a brief metaphor to the actual campaign workflow.
- Avoid: Letting a dramatic analogy replace the explanation of what the product does.
- Still open: Protocol mechanism, intended task, mainnet status, news context, footage provenance and audio remain unverified.
