## Lessons

- Keep: Use short recurring text to hold a varied montage together.
- Try: Build a Tin montage around three short task-specific lines, ending on the campaign they describe.
- Try: Add a readable creation-to-first-customer sequence after the emotional opening.
- Avoid: Avoid treating an isolated revenue chart as a clear product explanation.
- Still open: Exact product scope, revenue context, footage origins, transition details and all audio remain unverified.
