# 252 · Sam | Sam: The First AI Caretaker for Seniors

**Product from the film:** Sam is a small home companion device shown giving reminders and sharing daily-activity updates about an older adult.

**Explains well:** A single day connects cooking, breakfast, medication, gardening and a family dinner around the same person and device.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the product inside one continuous human day, with task moments that support the final payoff.
- Try: Follow a Tin user through a launch day, showing brief helpful actions before the team sees the finished campaign.
- Try: Pair an automatic update with a visible recipient and an easy control for what is shared.
- Avoid: Avoid implying reliable observation from an activity notification without showing how it is confirmed.
- Still open: Activity confirmation, sharing controls, missed-reminder handling, hardware behavior, later dialogue and all audio remain unverified.
