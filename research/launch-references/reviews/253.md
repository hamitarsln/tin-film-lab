## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the isolated hat reveal and repeated hat-wearing figures to explain form.
- Try: Show a wearer thinking one labelled command, then the same screen changing, with enough hold to read the result.
- Try: Test a shorter opening that moves from the reader to brain interface in a hat by 8 s, using the remaining time for setup and one use case.
- Avoid: Avoid replacing the promised control action with only aspirational imagery.
- Still open: Audio is unverified; the actual brain-control mechanism and supported actions are not tested.
