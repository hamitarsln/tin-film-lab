## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the ordinary Raise Cam footage beside a large outcome and time period.
- Try: Build a three-card sequence for one founder: application, live pitch, funding result, with the same name throughout.
- Try: Test a final card showing the next show time and application route against the current DONT WAIT and logo ending.
- Avoid: Avoid letting a generic action montage replace the entry and funding steps.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; funding amounts, eligibility and the exact investment mechanism remain unverified.
