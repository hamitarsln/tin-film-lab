## Lessons

- Keep: Plant a visible detail and return to its source at the moment it becomes useful.
- Try: Show Tin recalling an earlier campaign constraint with the exact brief excerpt beside the draft.
- Try: Add a clear confirmation card before an assistant publishes or orders anything in the story.
- Avoid: Avoid turning an observed detail into an unsupported personal conclusion.
- Still open: Observation controls, stored context, evidence quality, action approval, food-order completion and all audio remain unverified.
