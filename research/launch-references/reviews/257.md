# 257 · Content Rewards | Physical Billboards Are Dead: $10k Experiment #1

**Product from the film:** Content Rewards presents a way to fund creator-posted content campaigns and track their reach.

**Explains well:** The film turns a marketing claim into a visible comparison with spend, counting tools, campaign setup and result totals.

**Still unclear:** It treats counted passers-by and content views as comparable reach, without explaining attention, unique people, audience quality or business outcomes.

## Lessons

- Keep: Expose the comparison setup and measurement artifacts before showing the conclusion.
- Try: Compare two Tin campaign approaches with the same defined goal, budget and attribution window.
- Try: Show a compact table separating views, unique viewers, clicks and qualified outcomes beside the result graphic.
- Avoid: Avoid converting visibility counters into claims about unique people or equivalent attention.
- Still open: Spend currency and full costs, counting accuracy, unique reach, attribution, comparable outcomes and all audio remain unverified.
