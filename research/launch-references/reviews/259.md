## Lessons

- Keep: End a broad theme with a short, clear feature name.
- Try: Use a brief cultural opening for Tin, then show one complete campaign action before the CTA.
- Try: Compare a promise-only ending with an ending that includes the finished artifact and next step.
- Avoid: Avoid making the entire product explanation depend on repurposed dialogue.
- Still open: Exact market duration, order flow, settlement, source provenance, altered dialogue and all audio remain unverified.
