## Lessons

- Keep: Follow one recommendation into a visible real-world action.
- Try: Show Tin suggesting one campaign direction based on a named preference, then the user choosing and using it.
- Try: End with a specific feedback prompt that will improve the next recommendation.
- Avoid: Avoid skipping mutual confirmation and setup while implying the service handled them.
- Still open: Preference collection, match acceptance, scheduling, feedback use, later conversation and all audio remain unverified.
