## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the clear link between strategy, timing, narrative and on-site production.
- Try: Replace 15 seconds of metaphor footage with a day-by-day agenda and one finished output per day.
- Try: Follow one founder from a rough launch brief to revised plan and final filmed story, retaining Lake Como as brief setting footage.
- Avoid: Avoid using spacecraft success imagery as the only picture of the program result.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; actual participant footage, delivered assets and launch performance remain unverified.
