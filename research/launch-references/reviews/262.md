## Lessons

- Keep: Return to an early artifact after the work changes its meaning.
- Try: Show an original Tin campaign brief, the team’s decisions and the finished result, then return to the brief to explain what changed.
- Try: Use physical notes or source material to explain one creative choice rather than adding a broad mood montage.
- Avoid: Avoid making viewers wait through a long portfolio before learning the practical offer.
- Still open: Individual production roles, career claims, clip provenance, future collaboration terms, musical structure and all audio remain unverified.
