# 263 · Midnight | Midnight: Privacy by Default on Blockchain

**Product from the film:** Midnight presents itself as a privacy service or network linked to blockchain concerns.

**Explains well:** The film makes the risk of exposed activity feel personal through news, browsing and payment images.

**Still unclear:** It never explains which data Midnight protects or what a user does to gain that protection.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep selective text highlighting to make dense source material readable.
- Try: Use one wallet transaction example with labelled public and private fields, then show the users chosen disclosure.
- Try: Replace the final symbolic choice with a short product screen and one first action before the URL.
- Avoid: Avoid implying that broad privacy messaging proves prevention of the harms shown in news clips.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; product mechanism, protection scope and the news-to-product causal claims remain unverified.
