## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the change from individual event cards to a combined-bet summary.
- Try: Use the same basketball, political and Bitcoin selections in the parlay, moving each card into a matching row.
- Try: Replace ten seconds of broad montage with a held example explaining that the combined outcomes must meet the parlay rule, keeping payout labelled estimated.
- Avoid: Avoid swapping to unrelated selections between choice and summary.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; payout calculation, settlement rules and actual product behaviour remain unverified.
