# 265 · Andustry | Andustry: AI to Buy and Sell Industrial Goods Worldwide

**Product from the film:** Andustry presents industrial sourcing from a natural-language request through supplier quotes and offer acceptance.

**Explains well:** The same request progresses to ranked suppliers, comparable offers, a counteroffer and a final accepted quote.

**Still unclear:** The film negotiates one supplier’s price but selects another without explaining the price-versus-lead-time choice.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep named options visible from discovery through the final choice.
- Try: Show three Tin creative options with the same labels through comparison, revision and approval.
- Try: Add a short decision reason when the user selects an option other than the one just improved.
- Avoid: Avoid leaving the reason for the final selection implicit after highlighting a different option.
- Still open: Supplier verification, ranking criteria, outreach, quote timing, actual negotiation, delivery and all audio remain unverified.
