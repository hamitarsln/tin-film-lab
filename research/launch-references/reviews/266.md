## Lessons

- Keep: Name the event formats over actual gathering imagery.
- Try: Hold cities, dates, a registration address and one project outcome.
- Try: For Tin, show a participant moving from a brief to a reviewed campaign draft.
- Avoid: Ending an attendance invitation without a clear next step.
- Still open: Event scale, dates, city coverage, participant outcomes, footage sources and audio remain unverified.
