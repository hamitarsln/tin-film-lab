## Lessons

- Keep: Use an arrow and oversized label to make a metaphor immediately readable.
- Try: Add a clear offer sentence and expected participant output before the application URL.
- Try: For Tin, follow the spectator contrast with a brief becoming an editable draft.
- Avoid: Relying on the post title to explain an otherwise abstract program teaser.
- Still open: Program details, eligibility, date meaning, outcome claims, footage sources and audio are unverified.
