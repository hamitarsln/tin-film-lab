## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the dinner interruption analogy and return to the mother-explanation frame.
- Try: Use a job-change signal for one buyer, highlight the relevant sentence in the generated message and show its reply.
- Try: Test a shorter opening that reaches the dinner interruption by 5 s, then keeps the family joke as the closing callback.
- Avoid: Avoid generic outreach templates when claiming the right message for a specific signal.
