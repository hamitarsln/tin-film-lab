# 269 · Whop | Introducing Whop Treasury: Earn 6% Yield on Earnings

**Product from the film:** Whop Treasury is presented as a way to move a balance into a yield-bearing treasury position.

**Explains well:** The transfer example shows amount entry, a review step, confirmation and a changed balance allocation.

**Still unclear:** The film moves from a small account transfer to a large 30-year growth illustration without clearly explaining the illustration’s assumptions.

## Lessons

- Keep: Show the review step and resulting state change in the same interface.
- Try: Show a Tin user approving a campaign change and then the updated campaign state.
- Try: Label projected outcomes separately from observed results, with the assumptions next to the chart.
- Avoid: Avoid letting a large forecast look like the immediate result of a small action.
- Still open: Yield conditions, chart assumptions, fees, transfer execution, withdrawal terms and all audio remain unverified.
