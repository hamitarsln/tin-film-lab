# 270 · Git for Video | We just built git for video editing

**Product from the film:** Vit adds branch, commit, merge and history controls to collaborative video editing inside DaVinci Resolve.

**Explains well:** The same people and footage from the opening collaboration problem appear in the editor demonstration, connecting the problem to the tool.

**Still unclear:** The demo names branches and merges but does not clearly show which person changed what or how competing changes are combined.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use the opening scene itself as the material in the product demo.
- Try: Label two separate contributions and show both retained after a merge.
- Try: For Tin, merge two approved campaign edits into one clearly labeled final view.
- Avoid: Treating operation labels as enough explanation of what a merge actually changes.
- Still open: Concurrent behavior, conflicts, retained edits, audio and viewer understanding are unverified.
