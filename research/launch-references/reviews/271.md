## Lessons

- Keep: Reuse one specific failure across the problem, data explanation and approval.
- Try: For Tin, show one failed workflow, the exact evidence that explains it and an approved correction.
- Try: End the correction with the same task succeeding before showing broader customer statistics.
- Avoid: Avoid replacing a readable task outcome with a long sequence of large performance claims.
- Still open: Actual repair, benchmark methods, customer-claim conditions and audio performance remain unverified.
