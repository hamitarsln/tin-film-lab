# 273 · Zora | Introducing Autosell: 20% auto-sell every time price doubles

**Product from the film:** Zora Autosell is presented as automatically selling part of a coin holding as its price reaches higher multiples.

**Explains well:** The repeated 2x, 4x and 8x states, autosold notices and balance view make the automatic-sale idea visible.

**Still unclear:** The film does not show how to enable the rule, define its reference price or inspect the remaining holding after each sale.

## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat trigger, action notice and updated state using the same visual anchor.
- Try: Show the rule toggle, reference value, sold quantity and remaining quantity.
- Try: For Tin, connect a deadline trigger to a reminder, response and updated status.
- Avoid: Using rising balances as a substitute for explaining the full state change.
- Still open: Rule settings, transaction details, execution, returns, audio and viewer understanding remain unverified.
