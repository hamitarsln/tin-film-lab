## Lessons

- Keep: Show the standing instruction that explains an assistant’s choice.
- Try: For Tin, place the campaign priority rule beside an agent’s proposed schedule change.
- Try: After approval, show the changed calendar or sent item instead of ending with a completion message.
- Avoid: Avoid letting a polished confirmation stand in for a visible result.
- Still open: Context sources, action receipts, permissions setup, compliance claims and heard voice/music remain unverified.
