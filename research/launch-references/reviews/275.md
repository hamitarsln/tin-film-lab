## Lessons

- Keep: Pair a daily time commitment with a clear physical use action.
- Try: For Tin, show a user starting a short routine and the resulting saved output.
- Try: Place one readable result before usage counts so adoption does not carry the whole proof burden.
- Avoid: Avoid treating total usage as proof of the promised improvement.
- Still open: Session operation, benefit evidence, claim conditions, funding and preorder totals, and all heard audio remain unverified.
