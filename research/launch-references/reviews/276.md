## Lessons

- Keep: Keep one car-detailing website across the full creation and improvement story.
- Try: Use a true conversion-rate chart with visitors, bookings, dates and percentage units, or relabel the shown dollar chart as revenue.
- Try: Add a short owner-task card after site creation showing service delivery and customer handling beside the AI marketing tasks.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting a dollar chart as conversion rate or equating a generated website with a complete operating business.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; income, adoption, conversion and AI-ranking claims remain unverified.
