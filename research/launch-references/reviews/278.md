## Lessons

- Keep: Put a concrete payment notice beside broad infrastructure claims.
- Try: Show a new business creating a request and receiving its matching payment notice.
- Try: For Tin, follow social proof with a brief, draft and review confirmation.
- Avoid: Stacking unrelated totals in place of one complete user action.
- Still open: Financial figures, testimonials, eligibility, live behavior, sources and audio remain unverified.
