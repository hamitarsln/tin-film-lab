## Lessons

- Keep: Keep named chapters with a concrete prompt and visible output for each major feature.
- Try: Open on the Alto logo request and three outputs, then introduce the host and the three update chapters.
- Try: Test opaque white UI cards against the cloud-backed transparent version, holding the template before/after with changed fields highlighted.
- Avoid: Avoid spending the first quarter on announcement language before showing the task.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; actual speed, brand matching and connector behaviour remain unverified.
