## Lessons

- Keep: Name the concrete infrastructure problems behind the product.
- Try: For Tin, pair a short origin story with the actual workflow that now solves the problem.
- Try: Show configuration, one launch action and a visible successful run in one continuous example.
- Avoid: Avoid spending most of a launch film proving an earlier product while leaving the new one unseen.
- Still open: Deployment flow, reliability, adoption claims and all heard audio remain unverified.
