# 282 · Harbor | Harbor: Electronic Data Capture Platform for Clinical Trials

**Product from the film:** Harbor builds clinical study databases from protocols, extracts source data and helps monitors compare fields with source documents.

**Explains well:** The film connects sponsor setup, site data entry and monitoring, using the same study and side-by-side source evidence.

**Still unclear:** A potential issue and confidence score are shown, but the film does not show a reviewer correcting or approving the extracted value.

## Lessons

- Keep: Give each role a concrete task and connect extracted fields to their source.
- Try: Show a low-confidence field being checked, corrected or approved and saved.
- Try: For Tin, link a campaign claim to its source and show the reviewer’s accepted revision.
- Avoid: Ending a review demonstration at the confidence score without the human decision.
- Still open: Extraction accuracy, time savings, compliance, trial outcomes, asset origins and audio remain unverified.
