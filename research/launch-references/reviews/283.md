## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one named person and one concrete opportunity through multiple assistant capabilities.
- Try: For Tin, keep one campaign visible while the assistant researches, drafts and prepares the launch.
- Try: Show the submission or publish confirmation before jumping to a later success notification.
- Avoid: Avoid skipping the action that connects a recommendation to the claimed next-stage result.
- Still open: Application handling, matching inputs, employer and interview data, outcomes, voice-note content and all audio remain unverified.
