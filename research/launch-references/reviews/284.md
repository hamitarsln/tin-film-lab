## Lessons

- Keep: Use a specific wrong object and right object to explain relevance.
- Try: Rerun the original contract query with the correct source and answer.
- Try: For Tin, follow an analogy with a corrected source-backed campaign draft.
- Avoid: Allowing a metaphor to serve as the only proof of the actual task.
- Still open: Retrieval quality, technical generalizations, context model behavior, production sources and audio remain unverified.
