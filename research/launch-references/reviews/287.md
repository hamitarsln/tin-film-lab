# 287 · Perplexity | Announcing Personal Computer: always-on local AI on Mac mini

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the audience clarification beside the source report before producing the deck.
- Try: Hold a generated revenue slide next to the source PDF and highlight matching figures and the requested brand style.
- Try: Show one returned SwiftUI candidate with the exact requested experience fields, then close on the phone notification.
- Avoid: Avoid using progress labels and artifact covers as the whole proof of completed work.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; draft quality, candidate matching, execution time and processing location remain unverified.
