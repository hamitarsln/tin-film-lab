## Lessons

- Keep: Use one enlarged visual detail to explain why an item was flagged.
- Try: For Tin, show the precise mismatch behind a campaign warning, then the user’s review.
- Try: Carry one item ID through proposed action, approval and confirmed result instead of switching to abstract success effects.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting a suspected match and a final enforcement decision as the same step.
- Still open: Case review, authorization status, actual takedowns, absolute performance claims and all heard audio remain unverified.
