## Lessons

- Keep: Show a structured issue report with reproduction steps and linked evidence.
- Try: For Tin, enlarge one failed workflow step before showing the generated report.
- Try: Keep the same failure title and source timestamp visible in the alert and report so the handoff is easy to follow.
- Avoid: Avoid shrinking the actual failure while giving more space to generic backlog imagery.
- Still open: Detection quality, setup time, integration coverage, claim accuracy and all heard audio remain unverified.
