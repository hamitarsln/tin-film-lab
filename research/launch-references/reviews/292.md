**Product from the film:** Kled pays people for recordings of ordinary activities used as AI training data, with Whop handling the shown payments.

## Lessons

- Keep: Make the final task list name the exact activities already shown.
- Try: Show one task requirement, recording submission, acceptance and payment in order.
- Try: For Tin, connect a submitted asset to a review card naming that same asset.
- Avoid: Waiting most of the runtime to explain why the ordinary actions matter.
- Still open: Upload review, eligibility, payment execution, earnings, data use terms and audio remain unverified.
