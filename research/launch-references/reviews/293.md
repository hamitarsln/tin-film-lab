**Explains well:** Dark machine close-ups and marked material samples make the engineering ambition tangible.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use a real-looking physical output to make a broad mission concrete, while stating its evidence limits.
- Try: For Tin, label the working prototype and show one complete workflow before the future roadmap.
- Try: Place a measurable result beside the final artifact so its meaning is clear without a long founder explanation.
- Avoid: Avoid blending future concept imagery and present prototype evidence without labels.
- Still open: Material and test conditions, machine continuity, hotel imagery status, launch timing and all heard audio remain unverified.
