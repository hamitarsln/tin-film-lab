# 294 · ZeroSettle | ZeroSettle: drop-in SDK for direct billing to skip App Store fees

**Product from the film:** ZeroSettle helps mobile apps move subscribers to direct billing and configure retention offers.

**Explains well:** The film shows both the subscriber checkout and the developer controls behind offers.

**Still unclear:** It does not show how the old subscription ends or how the SDK is installed, despite the quick-setup promise.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep subscriber checkout and developer targeting in the same film.
- Try: Follow one JustOne plan from its dashboard discount setting to the phone offer, payment and old/new billing status.
- Try: Replace five seconds of the logo hold with the minimum SDK integration step and a clear next action for developers.
- Avoid: Avoid showing a subscription switch without explaining current charge and future billing timing.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; billing rules, fee applicability, integration time and retention results remain unverified.
