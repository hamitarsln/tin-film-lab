## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the clear shift from noisy paperwork to a clean licensing workspace.
- Try: Follow one state requirement from change alert through assigned task, populated filing and submitted status.
- Try: Replace the generic all-states chat with a close-up showing which filing is complete and what remains due.
- Avoid: Avoid using newspaper styling or a blanket chat answer as the main evidence of compliance.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Regulatory coverage, filing outcomes, cost and speed claims remain unverified.
