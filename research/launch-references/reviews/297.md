## Lessons

- Keep: Name the legacy systems and infrastructure tasks explicitly.
- Try: Demonstrate one permitted record update, generated calls and saved result.
- Try: For Tin, trace one approved asset into a connected system with its identifier and status.
- Avoid: Explaining the central mechanism only through a generic checklist.
- Still open: Generated-call behavior, supported systems, reliability, scale, customer claims and audio remain unverified.
