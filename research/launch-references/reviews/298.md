## Lessons

- Keep: Use varied close-up actions to make an abstract data need understandable.
- Try: For Tin, show the raw customer input beside the useful output it helps create.
- Try: Explain participation with one real submission, acceptance criteria and an observable result.
- Avoid: Avoid promising earnings before showing what a valid contribution involves.
- Still open: Capture method, dataset provenance and scale, quality review, income terms and all heard audio remain unverified.
