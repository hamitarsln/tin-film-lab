## Lessons

- Keep: Anchor the physical object while software outputs spread around it and regroup.
- Try: Show one button press leading to an exact saved reminder.
- Try: For Tin, spread drafts around a brief and regroup them into a review board.
- Avoid: Letting a collection of plausible notes replace one traceable capture result.
- Still open: Capture behavior, privacy, haptics, hardware properties, exact easing and audio remain unverified.
