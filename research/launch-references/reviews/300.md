## Lessons

- Keep: Start with the specific moment when the customer’s problem occurs.
- Try: Show account connection, portfolio choice and resulting state with clear comparison assumptions.
- Try: For Tin, compare drafts generated from the same stated campaign inputs.
- Avoid: Making the largest benefit number much more visible than its basis and the customer action.
- Still open: Return assumptions and results, comparative claims, fees, access, portfolio behavior and audio remain unverified.
