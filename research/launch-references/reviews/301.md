## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the maintenance caller through pickup, routing and human escalation.
- Try: Replace the generic security policy with a short urgent-leak rule, then show the resulting maintenance handoff and logged outcome.
- Try: Reuse the same caller and request in the later transcript screen instead of switching between budget, upgrade and refund examples.
- Avoid: Avoid relying on generic connected cards to explain policy-driven decisions.
- Still open: Audio is unheard; candidate ASR supports script structure only. Voice quality, language accuracy, performance and security remain unverified.
