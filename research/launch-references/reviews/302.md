## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same portrait through the beanie and reference-background changes.
- Try: Cut four seconds from the pen introduction and use them to show the missing summer and dusk instructions.
- Try: Add a brief before/after hold with the selected region and instruction visible for each edit.
- Avoid: Avoid showing several attractive outputs so fast that their input actions disappear.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Generation latency, repeatability and editing fidelity remain unverified.
