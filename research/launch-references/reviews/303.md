## Lessons

- Keep: Connect a physical source, such as shelves, to the resulting organized digital view.
- Try: Trace one book from shelf photo to catalog to student use.
- Try: For Tin, trace one source asset from capture to organized record to selected campaign use.
- Avoid: Jumping from administrative automation to reading outcomes without the intervening user action.
- Still open: Identification accuracy, staffing statistics, time savings, reading outcomes and audio remain unverified.
