## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat clocks and financial objects to maintain a coherent theme across varied footage.
- Try: Show one relevant trading control and its confirmation state.
- Try: For Tin, show a reviewer choice, setting change and approved revision.
- Avoid: Treating a manifesto about control as an explanation of the actual product.
- Still open: Product function, intended next action, trading claims, source rights and audio remain unverified.
