## Lessons

- Keep: Make the same imaginative motif become the final product artifact.
- Try: Show one track selection or saved-tune state in the generated app.
- Try: For Tin, carry one campaign idea into the exact final image, headline and preview.
- Avoid: Assuming a displayed app establishes its interaction behavior.
- Still open: App behavior, generation process, visual transformation mechanics and all audio qualities remain unverified.
