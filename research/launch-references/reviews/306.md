## Lessons

- Keep: Use one business scenario to connect several output formats.
- Try: Show the completed reservation after the voice request.
- Try: For Tin, open related campaign assets from one list and finish on the published result.
- Avoid: Treating a voice interface and request as proof of a finished action.
- Still open: Reservation completion, benchmark conditions, generation speed and audio qualities remain unverified.
