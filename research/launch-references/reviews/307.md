## Lessons

- Keep: Give an unfamiliar category a plain name and one personal example.
- Try: Show a friend updating the shared side-quest tracker.
- Try: For Tin, complete one request-to-editable-artifact sequence before showing the wider range.
- Avoid: Letting fast examples replace the final collaborative action.
- Still open: Sharing behavior, privacy implementation, report quality, creation limits and audio qualities remain unverified.
