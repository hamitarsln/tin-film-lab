## Lessons

- Keep: Use labeled real-world questions to explain a market interface.
- Try: Follow one event through a confirmed order to its position.
- Try: For Tin, turn one campaign signal into an edit, approval and published result.
- Avoid: Using speed imagery as a substitute for a complete action.
- Still open: Trading workflow completion, platform access, performance claims and all audio qualities remain unverified.
