## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the matching clip title and scheduled time across chat and final post.
- Try: Add three short labels: request, clip selection and scheduled post, with tight crops on the active interface.
- Try: Replace part of the chat wait with the chosen source range and one sentence explaining why that clip was selected.
- Avoid: Avoid making viewers decode several app names and tiny status messages to understand the product.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Actual publishing, clip quality, processing time and engagement outcomes remain unverified.
