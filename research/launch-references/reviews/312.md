## Lessons

- Keep: Keep clearly labelled ingredients as the story structure.
- Try: Replace ten seconds of laboratory handling with a brief-to-website sequence and one editable design choice.
- Try: Hold one final site beside the generic opening example and point to two concrete improvements in hierarchy and content.
- Avoid: Avoid letting a cinematic metaphor carry the whole explanation of an unfamiliar tool.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Product workflow, training claims, output value and performance remain unverified.
