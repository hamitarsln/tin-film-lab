# 315 · Lexius | Lexius turns security cameras into AI guards that detect theft

**Product from the film:** Lexius adds incident detection, phone alerts, footage search and case tracking to existing security cameras.

**Explains well:** The same shop scene appears first without and then with tracking overlays, followed by a phone alert and review clip.

**Still unclear:** The film jumps from detection to broader case and return-person claims without showing how a person reviews or corrects an alert.

## Lessons

- Keep: Reuse the same source scene through detection and notification.
- Try: Show a reviewer confirming or dismissing an alert and its case update.
- Try: For Tin, take one warning through preview evidence, correction and updated post.
- Avoid: Equating a detection alert with prevention or confirmed wrongdoing.
- Still open: Reviewer workflow, matching accuracy, compatibility, prevention outcomes and audio qualities remain unverified.
