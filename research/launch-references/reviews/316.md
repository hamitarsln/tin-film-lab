# 316 · Verdex | Verdex: Real-time satellite imagery audits for insurance claims

**Explains well:** The founders name the user, manual-inspection problem and two input sources, then connect them to remote claim settlement.

## Lessons

- Keep: Name the buyer, costly task and concrete input sources.
- Try: Show one field audit with evidence and a readable conclusion.
- Try: For Tin, connect brief and campaign data to an exact edit and preview.
- Avoid: Letting illustrative success cards stand in for the underlying decision.
- Still open: Actual audit workflow, claim evidence, regulatory and business claims, and all audio qualities remain unverified.
