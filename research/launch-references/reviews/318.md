## Lessons

- Keep: Start a recommendation demo with a precise audience and business goal.
- Try: For Tin, enter a specific partner profile and inspect one candidate’s supporting evidence.
- Try: Carry that candidate through an approved outreach and a logged response before showing aggregate revenue.
- Avoid: Avoid letting existing dashboards look like immediate results of a newly generated recommendation.
- Still open: Fit-score basis, prediction and attribution accuracy, service scope, revenue claims and all heard audio remain unverified.
