## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the visible exception and Delegate to agent action.
- Try: Trim ten seconds of the setup and show the same exception reach resolved status with its matched invoice.
- Try: Enlarge the agent checklist and keep the case identifier visible through every step, including the final notification.
- Avoid: Avoid cutting from an in-progress agent step to a completed-result claim.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Resolution accuracy, autonomy, speed and experience claims remain unverified.
