# 320 · Okara | Introducing Okara: an AI that respects your privacy

**Product from the film:** Okara presents itself as private AI chat that lets a person use AI without exposing their work to an imagined data auction.

**Explains well:** The glass-room auction turns personal data exposure into a visible scene; opening Okara removes the writer and his desk from the auction display.

**Still unclear:** The film explains the privacy promise through a metaphor but gives no concrete view of which chat data is protected or what the privacy boundary includes.

## Lessons

- Keep: Build a repeated visual rule and let the product action break it.
- Try: Add a plain, verified statement of the chat privacy boundary.
- Try: For Tin, resolve a repeated manual-review image with one visible published outcome.
- Avoid: Treating the auction metaphor as factual evidence of data sales or privacy implementation.
- Still open: Exact privacy scope, chat behavior, visual production methods and audio qualities remain unverified.
