## Lessons

- Keep: Make the initial request become the object of the story’s next action.
- Try: Show an edit or reservation action in the generated site.
- Try: For Tin, turn a character’s campaign request into the exact preview they approve and publish.
- Avoid: Allowing exaggerated sales dialogue to replace functional evidence.
- Still open: Generation timing, working site behavior, actual pricing/earnings and audio qualities remain unverified.
