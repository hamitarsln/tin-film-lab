# 324 · Perplexity | Introducing Perplexity Computer

**Product from the film:** Perplexity Computer runs multi-step research and creation tasks, returns files and apps, and sets up follow-up monitoring.

**Explains well:** Three examples show explicit requests followed by distinct outputs: a model and web app, a captioned clip, and a property model with monitoring.

**Still unclear:** The first example asks many things at once, while the outputs move too quickly to trace one source or change through the finished model.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep explicit prompts, concrete artifacts and follow-up scheduling.
- Try: Trace one analyst estimate into a labelled workbook row with old/new values and its source.
- Try: Shorten the final breadth montage and use that time to hold one complete output at readable scale.
- Avoid: Avoid cropping away metric names while asking viewers to appreciate highlighted model changes.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Financial conclusions, data quality, generation speed and monitoring outcomes remain unverified.
