## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the enlarged vector-point refinement before the mockups.
- Try: Add an SVG export and reopen step with path selection visible.
- Try: Hold two labelled variations side by side before choosing the one used on the storefront and billboard.
- Avoid: Avoid treating styled mockups as proof of an editable exported asset.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. SVG structure, generation speed and production-file quality remain unverified.
