## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the text-led optimism arc and game-pixel transition.
- Try: Replace one collage beat with a creator publishing an offer and a customer buying it through the platform.
- Try: Expand the final card with one plain sentence explaining the partnership and a clear next action.
- Avoid: Avoid treating famous creator imagery as self-explanatory evidence of a platform role.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Economic claims, creator relationships and investment details remain unverified.
