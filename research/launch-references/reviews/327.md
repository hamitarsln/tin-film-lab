# 327 · Typeless | Typeless 1.0: Voice Notes to Polished Prose, Not Just Transcription

**Product from the film:** Typeless is a mobile voice keyboard for dictation, spoken edits and translation inside other apps.

**Explains well:** The same text is dictated, translated, reformatted and shortened, making the edit chain easy to compare.

**Still unclear:** Repeated explanations delay the next action, and small text makes it hard to judge exactly what was removed or preserved.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same content through dictation and successive voice edits.
- Try: Cut repeated explanations and show each command immediately beside a before/after text comparison.
- Try: Zoom into the pen and Translate gestures, then hold the resulting changed sentence instead of the whole phone screen.
- Avoid: Avoid making viewers wait through repeated capability claims while the screen remains unchanged.
- Still open: Audio is unheard. Speech accuracy, translation nuance, compatibility and actual processing latency remain unverified.
