# 328 · Bitmor | We build a habit for your future. Stack sats with Bitmor

**Product from the film:** Bitmor presents automatic daily Bitcoin accumulation with goals, streaks and milestones.

**Explains well:** The setup shows a daily amount, a Bitcoin target, a token choice and a plan-start action.

**Still unclear:** A $10-a-day, ten-year example jumps to $1,000,000 without a visible explanation of the assumed price growth.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use a simple progress ring and milestones to explain a recurring routine.
- Try: For Tin, show a streak of completed review tasks with a clear definition of what counts.
- Try: Keep projected campaign outcomes separate from completed actions and display the forecast assumptions.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting a large future total as the automatic consequence of a small recurring input.
- Still open: Purchase execution, fees and network costs, custody, growth assumptions, availability and all heard audio remain unverified.
