## Lessons

- Keep: Use a side-by-side task with visible timing to make a speed claim concrete.
- Try: For Tin, compare the same campaign task with identical inputs and clear completion criteria.
- Try: Add a short output-quality check next to the faster result before moving to other features.
- Avoid: Avoid letting speed alone imply equivalent output quality.
- Still open: Timing setup, geometry equivalence, constraints, tolerances, drawing correctness, adoption and all heard audio remain unverified.
