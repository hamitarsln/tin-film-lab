# 330 · Xbox | Original Xbox Launch Day - November 15th, 2001

**Product from the film:** Xbox is presented as a home game console with a controller, DVD remote kit and games, received as a surprise at home.

**Explains well:** The package display is established before the recipient arrives, so the later reaction has a clear object and payoff.

**Still unclear:** The clip ends before setup or play; it communicates receiving the console more clearly than the experience of using it.

## Lessons

- Keep: Establish the object before showing its recipient’s discovery.
- Try: Continue into one first-use action.
- Try: For Tin, show a client opening the finished campaign and making a concrete choice or publishing it.
- Avoid: Treating visible excitement as evidence of product performance.
- Still open: First-use experience, historical provenance, exact date and audio qualities remain unverified.
