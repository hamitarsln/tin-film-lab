## Lessons

- Keep: Use one identity to connect several output formats on a shared canvas.
- Try: Show one shared brand correction updating related designs.
- Try: For Tin, show image, caption and schedule preview together and apply one review correction.
- Avoid: Treating many moving labels as a measured speed or quality result.
- Still open: Agent roles, user review, shared corrections, export behavior and audio qualities remain unverified.
