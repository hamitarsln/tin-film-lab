## Lessons

- Keep: Keep readable task examples before the mosaic becomes a scale image.
- Try: For a robotics-data film, carry one sewing clip through capture, hand data and a labelled training output.
- Try: Shorten the fine-grid section and use the time to show who uses the resulting data.
- Avoid: Avoid using more tiles as the only explanation of product value.
- Still open: Audio is unverified; recording origins, point accuracy and downstream training results are not checked.
