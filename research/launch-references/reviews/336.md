## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the repeated input, readable result and short feature-label structure.
- Try: Test a shorter cut with the time-correction, code-term and mixed-language cases, announcing Android before the first example.
- Try: Show one continuous input-to-output capture with an explicit success rule before adding a score.
- Avoid: Avoid letting the prize and multiple endings bury availability and the first-use action.
- Still open: Actual speech, rap fidelity, whisper level and music are unverified. Comparative accuracy and contest conditions are not independently checked.
