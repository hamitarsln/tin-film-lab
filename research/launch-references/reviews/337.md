## Lessons

- Keep: Show detection and classification as two separate visible states.
- Try: For Tin, mark possible issues first, then show the evidence that changes one to a confirmed category.
- Try: Keep source footage and system output synchronized with a shared timestamp during a demo.
- Avoid: Avoid treating an edited example as proof of a broad accuracy claim.
- Still open: Feed synchronization, target identity, continuous tracking, range, accuracy and all heard audio remain unverified.
