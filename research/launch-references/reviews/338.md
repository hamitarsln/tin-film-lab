# 338 · Reframe | Reframe Automates Hardware Procurement via BOM Upload and ERP

**Product from the film:** Reframe uses parts lists, email and Slack to help hardware teams plan and manage procurement.

**Explains well:** The dashboard and supplier-delay chat turn a broad procurement problem into specific parts and next steps.

**Still unclear:** The uploaded parts list is not traced to one final order or schedule change, and the problem setup takes over 30 seconds.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep specific quantities, suppliers and lead-time consequences in the product demonstration.
- Try: Start with one delayed part and use the dashboard and Slack thread to resolve that same case.
- Try: Cut the problem introduction to one table/email example and spend the saved time on approval and the resulting order state.
- Avoid: Avoid changing example parts between the recommendation and collaboration payoff.
- Still open: Actual audio, procurement execution and supplier responses are unverified; the schedule update is stated in chat but not displayed.
