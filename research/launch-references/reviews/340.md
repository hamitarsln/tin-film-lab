# 340 · Fern | Fern: Realtime AI Meeting Coach & Copilot

**Product from the film:** Fern is a meeting assistant that participates when invited, gives contextual suggestions and drafts follow-up text; the film also promises socially timed participation.

**Explains well:** A meeting discussion leads to an explicit question to Fern, a contextual recommendation and a requested cancellation-email draft.

**Still unclear:** The film promises proactive participation, but its main example begins with a direct request to Fern rather than showing it choose an appropriate moment itself.

## Lessons

- Keep: Let conversational help produce a specific editable artifact.
- Try: Show a genuinely unprompted suggestion and how a participant can decline it.
- Try: For Tin, turn a suggestion into a draft and show approval of the exact preview.
- Avoid: Using an invited answer as the sole evidence for proactive timing.
- Still open: Turn-taking, latency, proactive behavior, sent-email outcome, future integrations and audio qualities remain unverified.
