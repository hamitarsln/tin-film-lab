## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same-task manual-versus-robot comparison before showing detailed inspection labels.
- Try: Open on manual inspection beside the automated concept, then explain the farm context in one short scene.
- Try: Track one fish from measurement to a visible grade and routing decision, with the shown time labelled accurately.
- Avoid: Avoid letting sector statistics consume most of the time before the product appears.
- Still open: Audio, hardware deployment, speed, accuracy and commercial claims are not independently checked; imagery origin is uncertain.
