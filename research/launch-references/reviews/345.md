# 345 · Forum | Forum: Regulated Exchange to Trade on Attention

**Product from the film:** Forum presents a market where users take long or short positions on the online attention around topics and cultural trends.

**Explains well:** A consistent points/price/chart card overlays several recognizable topics, making attention into a recurring tradable object.

**Still unclear:** The film does not explain how attention points are measured or how they relate to the displayed market price and a position’s outcome.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use one repeated card to make an unfamiliar category concrete.
- Try: Explain one attention measure, entry price and resulting position change.
- Try: For Tin, connect a defined trend signal to a campaign decision and published artifact.
- Avoid: Letting rising charts stand in for an explained measurement and outcome.
- Still open: Attention calculation, pricing relationship, trade completion, legal status, returns and audio qualities remain unverified.
