## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same artifact on screen as controls are introduced.
- Try: For Tin, show one prompt and its generated result before adding controls.
- Try: Label one input gesture or slider change and hold the corresponding output state long enough to compare.
- Avoid: Avoid relying on simultaneous input and animation as proof of a clear causal relationship.
- Still open: Prompt, code, model authorship, gesture mapping, music response and all heard audio properties remain unverified.
