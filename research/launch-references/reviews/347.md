# 347 · Rork Max | Introducing Rork Max: AI that one-shots apps for all Apple platforms

**Product from the film:** Rork Max presents AI app creation for Apple devices, with installation and publishing controls.

**Explains well:** A consistent device-and-label layout makes the range of app types and platforms easy to follow.

**Still unclear:** No prompt-to-build sequence supports the one-shot promise, and the App Store headline jumps ahead of the visible publishing state.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the stable device-and-label layout and the visible installation confirmation.
- Try: Use one habit-app prompt, its generated screen and installation on the same phone as a complete mini-story.
- Try: Replace the App Store claim shot with the actual TestFlight submission flow and a clearly named resulting state.
- Avoid: Avoid implying store release from a menu that only shows submission options.
- Still open: Audio, app generation, fee requirements and store release are unverified; sampled activity does not establish every shown feature.
