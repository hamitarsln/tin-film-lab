## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the relationship-led opening and recurring flower motif.
- Try: Carry one inactive account through its usage/support evidence into a specific outreach draft.
- Try: Show the proposed QBR deck beside the source customer wins instead of only naming the feature.
- Avoid: Avoid replacing evidence and output with generic account labels and a recommendation panel.
- Still open: Audio, signal quality and automated execution are unverified; the panel shows a confirmation step.
