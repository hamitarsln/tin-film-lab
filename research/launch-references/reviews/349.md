## Lessons

- Keep: Keep explicit receivable/payable chapters and readable one-action panel layouts.
- Try: Trace the Acme PO request through the corrected invoice and confirmed portal submission.
- Try: Show one real discrepancy, its resolution and the final ledger entry, using time from the long end card.
- Avoid: Avoid using generic green states as visual support for a different named action.
- Still open: Audio and financial execution are unverified; captions claim more than the visible final states establish.
