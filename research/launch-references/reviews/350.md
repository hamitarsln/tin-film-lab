# 350 · RoboDock | RoboDock: Robots Automating AV Depot Operations

**Explains well:** A labelled real demo follows a red car through parking, inspection, port detection, charging and departure.

## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat the proposed workflow in a labelled real demonstration.
- Try: For Tin, place a short real run immediately after the animated workflow overview.
- Try: Show one failed check and the resulting blocked action beside the successful path.
- Avoid: Avoid letting broad compatibility claims outrun the cases actually shown.
- Still open: Cost basis, inspection accuracy, compatibility range, charge duration, image provenance and audio properties remain unverified.
