## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the physical privacy action followed by a simple feature toggle.
- Try: After the blind closes, show one labelled product example of information hidden by Incognito.
- Try: Hold the Incognito name longer and add a brief user-task phrase below it.
- Avoid: Avoid assuming that a clear metaphor also explains an unfamiliar product.
- Still open: Audio and actual privacy behaviour are unverified; the footage source is not identified.
