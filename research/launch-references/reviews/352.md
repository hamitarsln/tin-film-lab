## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat the exact failed requests after introducing the enabling feature.
- Try: Follow one paid API call to returned data and matching receipt.
- Try: For Tin, repeat a blocked campaign after account connection and show publication.
- Avoid: Using balance and revenue changes as the only visible completion of a task.
- Still open: Transaction completion, account setup, gateway behavior and all audio qualities remain unverified.
