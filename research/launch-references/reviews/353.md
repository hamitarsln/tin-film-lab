# 353 · Contra Payments | Introducing Contra Payments: Sell to AI Agents

**Product from the film:** Contra Payments lets creative sellers offer products and services to people and their AI agents.

**Explains well:** The brand-kit example carries a named seller and product through agent selection, checkout and a confirmation.

**Still unclear:** The jump from one purchase to public reviews, better discovery and agent-demand scores is not explained with a traceable rule.

## Lessons

- Keep: Keep a named product and seller consistent through the agent purchase and confirmation.
- Try: Add one clear state showing how the confirmed purchase affects the seller’s verified revenue or review eligibility.
- Try: Make a shorter seller cut ending on Create product, and a separate spreadsheet-promotion cut.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting a demand score or growing profile metric without showing what it measures.
- Still open: Audio, settlement, market-size claims and demand-score method are unverified; product UI is evidence of the film’s example only.
