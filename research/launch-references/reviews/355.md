## Lessons

- Keep: Make the visual behavior of a control word illustrate its meaning.
- Try: For a narration feature, animate Dynamics as a growing and shrinking text column, then compare two labeled audio outputs in a validated listening pass.
- Try: For image-to-music, retain one uploaded image beside its prompt and named track player through generation so the input/result link stays visible.
- Avoid: Do not let a long range-of-capabilities montage replace the first usable workflow.
- Still open: Audio quality, music changes, sync, watermark behavior and current user access remain unchecked.
