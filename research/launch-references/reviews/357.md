# 357 · Grade | Grade: Performance-Based Payroll, $380k Paid in 30 Days

**Product from the film:** Grade ties creator and other worker payments to measured performance.

**Explains well:** A rate field leads to creator performance cards and payment confirmations, making the payment idea visible.

**Still unclear:** The shown $4 per 1,000 views does not explain the following 25K-view/$1,000 payout example; the film may be switching examples without saying so.

## Lessons

- Keep: Put the payment rule directly before the worker payout cards.
- Try: Show one consistent rate × performance calculation and matching payment.
- Try: For Tin, connect one metric and rule to a visible campaign action before multiplying examples.
- Avoid: Switching numerical examples without explaining the changed basis.
- Still open: Tracking method, payout basis, actual payments, scale claims and all audio qualities remain unverified.
