## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same page and card into an editable canvas before showing variants.
- Try: Open with the browser/canvas result and finish with the chosen variant running in code.
- Try: For Tin, compare campaign directions on a canvas and carry the chosen one into publication.
- Avoid: Delaying the visible feature until after a long uncaptioned discussion.
- Still open: Setup, capture fidelity, real-time duration, return-to-code behavior and audio qualities remain unverified.
