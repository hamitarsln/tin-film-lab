## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same recognizable asset from the request into the output showcase.
- Try: For Tin, keep a source image beside the generated creative for a direct comparison.
- Try: Write out a voice request and show the scheduled task record before showing its delivered summary.
- Avoid: Avoid introducing several tool outputs faster than their inputs can be understood.
- Still open: Voice request, recurring execution, connected-tool setup, output provenance and all audio properties remain unverified.
