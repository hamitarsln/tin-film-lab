## Lessons

- Keep: Use a clear physical product action to motivate a change into a fictional world.
- Try: For Tin, let a real button press lead into a short visual metaphor, then return to the completed output.
- Try: Show the product name before the story expands, and end with a practical use close-up beside the buying prompt.
- Avoid: Avoid leaving the real product role and brand hidden for most of a story-led film.
- Still open: Exact keycap functions, compatibility, materials, animation details between samples and all sound properties remain unverified.
