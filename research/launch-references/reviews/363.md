## Lessons

- Keep: Show named workflow stages and an inspectable output such as a pull request.
- Try: For a Tin outreach demo, retain one contact through drafted email, approval, sent state and a labeled response; keep status labels consistent.
- Try: For a repository workflow, show prompt, completed change summary and one readable diff, then explicitly label the user review step.
- Avoid: Avoid showing internal sending activity under an Idle heading or switching workflows before a clear result.
- Still open: Workflow execution, delivered emails, ranking impact, claimed backing and all audio remain unverified.
