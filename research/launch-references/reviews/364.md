## Lessons

- Keep: Show a recognizable structure progressing from foundation to roof.
- Try: Connect one castle preview to placement and a final in-game walk-through.
- Try: For Tin, carry one generated asset into its actual channel placement.
- Avoid: Treating a broad montage as an explanation of the apply step.
- Still open: Generation speed, placement workflow, visual fidelity, multiplayer support and audio qualities remain unverified.
