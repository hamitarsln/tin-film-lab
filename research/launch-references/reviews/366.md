## Lessons

- Keep: Pair a technical claim with the physical instrument or material it concerns.
- Try: Trace one thermal profile through an identified experiment to its measurement.
- Try: For Tin, link a recommendation, changed setting and matching campaign result.
- Avoid: Using lab atmosphere as the sole bridge between a recommendation and a numerical result.
- Still open: Scientific result, experimental method, actual automation, exact UI details and audio qualities remain unverified.
