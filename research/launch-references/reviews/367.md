## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one simple object across changing environments and transform it at the ending.
- Try: For an AI training launch, pair one environment definition with a run status and labeled result before returning to the visual metaphor.
- Try: Try a three-shot closed-to-open object arc using identical framing, with the product name and action visible earlier.
- Avoid: Avoid letting a long science montage substitute for the first practical task.
- Still open: Training behavior, openness details, asset provenance, sound and exact montage transitions remain unverified.
