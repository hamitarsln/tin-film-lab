## Lessons

- Keep: Make a mid-task request visibly reappear in the final report.
- Try: Trace one report claim into its cited evidence before export.
- Try: For Tin, add a constraint during generation and highlight its effect in the final asset.
- Avoid: Equating a long sourced report with demonstrated expert-level accuracy.
- Still open: Research accuracy, source adherence, elapsed time, export completion and audio qualities remain unverified.
