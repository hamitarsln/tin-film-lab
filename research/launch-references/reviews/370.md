## Lessons

- Keep: Keep one sector-specific example through research and outreach.
- Try: For Tin, follow one recipient from research through an approved message to a response.
- Try: Connect the final calendar event to the exact reply that created it.
- Avoid: Avoid presenting a populated calendar as a complete explanation of how meetings were obtained.
- Still open: Data accuracy, sell-likelihood scoring, actual sends, replies, booked meetings and audio properties remain unverified.
