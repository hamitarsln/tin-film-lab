## Lessons

- Keep: Use a structured table to bridge the source document and target form.
- Try: Show the source/target setup and run trigger before the invoice demonstration.
- Try: For Tin, retain one campaign identifier through brief, asset and publishing confirmation.
- Avoid: Leaving workflow initiation implicit while spending many seconds on broad automation claims.
- Still open: Setup, actual delivery, extraction accuracy, speed comparison, mechanism and audio qualities remain unverified.
