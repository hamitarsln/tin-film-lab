## Lessons

- Keep: Use one persistent input control to connect several app-specific outputs.
- Try: For an email assistant, show the exact short request above the pill and highlight the matching sentence in the resulting draft.
- Try: Try one restrained perspective panel entrance, then hold it flat for reading and label Draft ready before any send action.
- Avoid: Avoid calling a visible draft a completed reply or hiding the instruction needed to judge the result.
- Still open: Actual voice recognition, personal style matching, compatibility, setup and all heard sound remain unverified.
