## Lessons

- Keep: Use two visible objects and hand positions to explain a spatial infrastructure problem.
- Try: Show deployment output and one completed browser invocation with measured duration.
- Try: For Tin, bring brief and execution artifacts into one workflow and show the completed step.
- Avoid: Ending the workflow explanation at a command without its result.
- Still open: Deployment behavior, latency, compatibility, customer claims and audio qualities remain unverified.
