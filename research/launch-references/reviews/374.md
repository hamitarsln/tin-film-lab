# 374 · Essential | Essential Apps: AI builds apps shaped around your needs

**Product from the film:** Essential Apps lets a user describe a personal app and deploy it to a Nothing phone.

**Explains well:** A marathon-training need becomes a prompt, a run-time widget and a deployment step before the user leaves in running clothes.

**Still unclear:** The film does not show how the app gains calendar and weather access or how those inputs produce its suggested times.

## Lessons

- Keep: Return the generated app to the same person and need that opened the story.
- Try: Show calendar/weather connection and the inputs behind one suggested run.
- Try: For Tin, connect an event-date constraint to a generated schedule and final artifact.
- Avoid: Making personalization look automatic without showing where its inputs come from.
- Still open: Data connections, installation, recommendation quality, precise transitions and audio qualities remain unverified.
