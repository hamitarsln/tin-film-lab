## Lessons

- Keep: End the search demonstration with a source passage and a concrete reason for its relevance.
- Try: For a Tin research report, keep one source ID highlighted from table row into excerpt and explanation, holding the excerpt flat long enough to read.
- Try: Try a brand-shaped black-to-white wipe at the problem/solution boundary, then use restrained UI motion for the evidence sequence.
- Avoid: Avoid replacing the source-selection step with an abstract network when traceability is the main promise.
- Still open: Search quality, claim accuracy, backing statement, asset provenance and all audio remain unverified.
