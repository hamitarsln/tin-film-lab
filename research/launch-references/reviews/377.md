## Lessons

- Keep: Use organism and lab imagery to ground an unfamiliar discovery strategy.
- Try: Trace one molecule through an assay result and clearly stated research stage.
- Try: For Tin, retain one source signal’s identifier through selection and evaluation.
- Avoid: Letting analogies to existing drugs stand in for a company-specific candidate result.
- Still open: Scientific claims, candidate validation/stage, AI role, asset provenance and audio qualities remain unverified.
