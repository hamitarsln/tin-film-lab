## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the request and the corresponding changed system record visible together.
- Try: For Tin, finish a multistep request with one receipt listing every requested outcome.
- Try: Use brighter text and fewer simultaneous cards when showing action details on a dark background.
- Avoid: Avoid claiming an end-to-end result while one original request has no clear final record.
- Still open: Actual integration, changed stay dates, real bookings, physical delivery, metric basis and heard audio properties remain unverified.
