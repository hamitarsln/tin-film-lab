## Lessons

- Keep: Show the exact format of the action requested after a short brand reveal.
- Try: For a workspace-name launch, pair the example URL with one concrete use and a visible reservation confirmation.
- Try: Try a shorter two-trail logo reveal, saving the time for a one-line explanation of who the account helps.
- Avoid: Avoid asking for a reservation without explaining what the reserved identity enables.
- Still open: Underlying product, reservation availability, AGI claim, sound and flash safety remain unverified.
