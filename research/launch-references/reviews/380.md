## Lessons

- Keep: End setup with a concrete question and a visible response in the destination app.
- Try: For Tin, label each setup stage and slow the exact approval action.
- Try: Show a larger final task result beside a compact elapsed-time label.
- Avoid: Avoid using a large stopwatch at the cost of readable setup steps.
- Still open: Setup prerequisites, omitted time, security properties, exact access correction, answer accuracy and all audio remain unverified.
