## Lessons

- Keep: Use one diplomatic decision to demonstrate a chain of consequences.
- Try: Show a typed proposal, submission, turn advance and matching response.
- Try: For Tin, trace one changed decision into schedule, asset and channel changes.
- Avoid: Explaining agency only through narrated consequences without the player’s input.
- Still open: Adaptive behavior, memory, game mechanics, reward model, scale claims and audio qualities remain unverified.
