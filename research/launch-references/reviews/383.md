# 383 · SurfCash | SurfCash Now Live on App Store: Make Crypto Spendable

**Product from the film:** SurfCash presents crypto spending through QR payments, with a self-custodial wallet.

**Explains well:** The $100 amount, QR graphic and completed-payment card give the offer a concrete payment sequence.

**Still unclear:** The film does not explain why $100 becomes $99.9, which countries are supported or how a user funds and confirms the payment.

## Lessons

- Keep: Use a clear amount-to-action-to-receipt sequence as the center of a financial product film.
- Try: For a payment demo, retain input amount, conversion or fee and recipient amount together until the receipt appears.
- Try: Try a shorter local-life montage followed by one user funding, scanning and confirming a payment before adding secondary benefits.
- Avoid: Avoid an unexplained amount change or a yield headline without the context needed to understand the offer.
- Still open: Transaction reality, fees, custody, yield conditions, reach, bank status, asset sources and all sound remain unverified.
