# 385 · Perplexity | Model Council: Run Three Frontier Models at Once

**Product from the film:** Perplexity Model Council runs a question through three selected models and compares their answers.

**Explains well:** The film shows where to enable the mode, select models and inspect agreements, disagreements and individual responses.

**Still unclear:** The dense output tables pass too quickly to show why one disagreement matters or what the user should do next.

## Lessons

- Keep: Expose both the combined comparison and the original model responses.
- Try: For a Tin research comparison, highlight one disagreement row, then open its two source passages before returning to the summary.
- Try: Try a compact three-model progress view followed by one agreement, one disagreement and one unresolved question, with enough hold time to read.
- Avoid: Avoid implying that three agreeing models establish accuracy or showing more text than the viewer can inspect.
- Still open: Economic output accuracy, source checks, model independence, performance and all audio remain unverified.
