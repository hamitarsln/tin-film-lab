## Lessons

- Keep: Use stable colored panels to organize a broad portfolio.
- Try: Finish the missed-flight conversation with a visible customer action/result.
- Try: For Tin, carry one campaign through distinct planning, creation and execution panels.
- Avoid: Treating a transcript or waveform as evidence of improved audio quality.
- Still open: Audio quality, voice timing, agent task completion, company claims and exact motion remain unverified.
