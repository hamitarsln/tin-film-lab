## Lessons

- Keep: Connect a familiar editing frustration to a concrete natural-language search result.
- Try: For a film assistant demo, retain one searched clip through the assembled timeline and play its place in the final story.
- Try: Try a before/after soundtrack comparison with the same video excerpt and labeled versions, then validate it through actual listening.
- Avoid: Avoid using a populated timeline or waveform as the only proof of story quality and music fit.
- Still open: Search quality, duration compliance, final playback, export, soundtrack originality and all heard sound remain unverified.
