## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same label position while changing visual worlds.
- Try: Show title selection, episode length and playback in one phone sequence.
- Try: For Tin, label three campaign styles consistently before showing selection.
- Avoid: Leaving “new type” as a slogan without a visible format difference.
- Still open: Audio, content origin, app behavior and commercial claims remain unverified.
