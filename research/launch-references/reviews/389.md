## Lessons

- Keep: Make the explanation and approval of a generated decision visible.
- Try: For Tin, follow one input constraint into one generated output and its reason.
- Try: Move a short product preview earlier, then spend the saved time on a readable review decision.
- Avoid: Avoid spending most of the film on the problem while rushing the distinctive review mechanism.
- Still open: Engineering correctness, standards compliance, timing claims, asset provenance and all heard audio properties remain unverified.
