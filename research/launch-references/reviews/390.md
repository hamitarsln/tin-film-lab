## Lessons

- Keep: Use a stable team composition for clear speaker handoffs.
- Try: Show one set and its matching automatic log.
- Try: For Tin, place a complete campaign example between the founder introduction and close.
- Avoid: Explaining a physical product without isolating it or its output.
- Still open: Tracking accuracy, health claims, device form, sound and viewer understanding remain unverified.
