## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one place name from interface to real-world action.
- Try: Try a Tin workflow film with the same campaign name on setup, active run and final result.
- Try: Try a hardware demo with a route overlay and a final wide shot showing the herd at the chosen destination.
- Avoid: Avoid replacing the final physical result with a floating completion notice.
- Still open: Audio delivery, actual autonomy, monitoring accuracy and source footage provenance remain open.
