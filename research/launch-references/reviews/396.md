## Lessons

- Keep: Give a creator challenge clear participants, a time window and stakes.
- Try: For Tin, follow one participant’s brief, revision and finished output within the community story.
- Try: Resolve the winner or result before moving to the sign-up prompt.
- Avoid: Avoid building suspense around a challenge result that the film never clearly shows.
- Still open: Winner, final creator outputs, production attribution, offer conditions and all heard sound properties remain unverified.
