## Lessons

- Keep: Show a recognizable physical input becoming an explorable result.
- Try: For Tin, carry one handmade or user-supplied asset from capture through creation to output.
- Try: Keep one interaction uncut with the input and response visible together.
- Avoid: Avoid letting short varied examples imply unlimited persistence or precise control.
- Still open: Generation time, control latency, session limits, long-term continuity, physics behavior and all audio properties remain unverified.
