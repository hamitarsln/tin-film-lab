## Lessons

- Keep: Show the review control inside the task rather than only making a trust claim.
- Try: For a Tin email demo, hold the addressed draft and approval button before the sent receipt.
- Try: For delegated shopping, compare the reference photo with a completed cart and budget total in one closing frame.
- Avoid: Ending the most complex task before its requested result.
- Still open: Actual audio, fast transitions, availability details and shopping completion remain unverified.
