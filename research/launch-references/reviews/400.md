## Lessons

- Keep: Return to the exact earlier work screen to close a character story.
- Try: Try a Tin film with the same campaign approval screen before and after a clear workflow action.
- Try: Try a wearable routine scene that shows start, 20-minute timer and completed session before the lifestyle montage.
- Avoid: Avoid using a smiling after-scene as proof of a health mechanism.
- Still open: Audio, health efficacy, mechanism, typical time to change and device session controls remain unverified.
