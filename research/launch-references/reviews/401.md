## Lessons

- Keep: Use one personal app idea to connect planning, testing and publishing.
- Try: Repeat the same flashcard interaction after the repair message.
- Try: For Tin, carry one named campaign through request, check, repair and publication.
- Avoid: Using a success message as the only visible repair payoff.
- Still open: Audio, repair behavior, integrations, publication and performance claims remain unverified.
