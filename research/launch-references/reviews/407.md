## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat one query-to-person pattern across distinct technical domains.
- Try: Open one ranked candidate and a matching contribution before outreach.
- Try: For Tin, connect a creator search to portfolio evidence and a message draft.
- Avoid: Jumping from query to a persuasive face without the selection evidence.
- Still open: Candidate authenticity, ranking quality, hiring outcomes and sound remain unverified.
