## Lessons

- Keep: Pair a polished template with an immediate visible customization.
- Try: For Tin brand templates, change a palette beside the preview and keep both states on screen.
- Try: Test an opening Editable animated cards label before the showcase, then compare new-viewer understanding.
- Avoid: Letting generic sample card copy disguise the actual product category.
- Still open: Export workflow, precise easing and all sound remain unverified.
