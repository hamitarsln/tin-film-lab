## Lessons

- Keep: Use a collection of examples as the material of the final brand reveal.
- Try: For Tin, form the closing mark from actual campaign outputs after showing two complete examples.
- Try: Test a short role statement before the logo so viewers can explain what the organization does.
- Avoid: Treating recognizable technology footage as proof of a brand’s contribution.
- Still open: Audio structure, clip rights, montage provenance and the service offer remain unverified.
