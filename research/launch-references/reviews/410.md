## Lessons

- Keep: Use a simple black closing field to make the brand readable after a dense montage.
- Try: Show a source-to-destination transfer example after the teleport phrase.
- Try: For Tin, limit the problem montage to three terms before a real workflow.
- Avoid: Using jargon and dramatic imagery as the entire product explanation.
- Still open: Product mechanism, finance claims, source provenance and sound remain unverified.
