## Lessons

- Keep: Pair surprising finished work with a visible making context.
- Try: Try a Tin showcase that shows one polished creative, its brief and two edit states.
- Try: Try a closing brand assembly using shapes already present in the showcased assets, with one clear focal point.
- Avoid: Avoid leaving every how-was-this-made question unanswered.
- Still open: Audio, exact construction methods, asset provenance and unobserved transition details remain open.
