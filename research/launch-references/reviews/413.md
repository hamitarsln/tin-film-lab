## Lessons

- Keep: Give testimonial clips a shared visual grammar while retaining human faces.
- Try: Try a Tin customer reel with three named mini-cases, each paired with its actual campaign output.
- Try: Try a community close where a repeated gesture becomes a brand motif, using a slower final cluster hold.
- Avoid: Avoid mixing unrelated completion times as if they describe the same task.
- Still open: Audio delivery, testimonial provenance, time claims and business outcomes remain unverified.
