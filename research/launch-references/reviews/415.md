## Lessons

- Keep: Show both context access and an action service inside one workflow.
- Try: For Tin, attach a brand board, create a draft and connect a publishing service before showing the published preview.
- Try: For this lead finder, submit one company URL and hold the resulting structured details beside the source.
- Avoid: Stopping at Connected when the opening task needs an actual output.
- Still open: Full setup requirements, app execution, wireframe fidelity and sound remain unverified.
