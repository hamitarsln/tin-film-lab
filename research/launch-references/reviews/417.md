## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same people and dog across varied outputs.
- Try: Show one original-request-result edit before the montage.
- Try: For Tin, keep a product and headline fixed while demonstrating three requested styles.
- Avoid: Letting output variety hide the user’s control over each change.
- Still open: Prompt fidelity, source process, generation time and all acoustic qualities remain unverified.
