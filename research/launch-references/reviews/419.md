## Lessons

- Keep: Show the exact text change inside the address field.
- Try: Add one concrete product descriptor under the final domain.
- Try: For Tin, show a renamed feature inside its real navigation context.
- Avoid: Expecting a domain announcement to teach a new viewer the product.
- Still open: Sound, live domain behavior and asset provenance remain unverified.
