## Lessons

- Keep: Return to the opening life stage at the end to complete the emotional idea.
- Try: For a Tin creator story, show an early rough campaign, the current work and a new creator starting with the product.
- Try: Compare the sports-only close with a short real service interaction before the final marks.
- Avoid: Using achievement imagery as a substitute for explaining the offer.
- Still open: Actual audio, footage provenance, athlete pairings and the joint service remain unverified.
