## Lessons

- Keep: Make the defect visible before explaining the repair.
- Try: For Tin, show a failed publish preview, the diagnosed field and the same publish action succeeding after the fix.
- Try: Compare overlapping agent cards with a three-column status view to test whether viewers understand parallel work.
- Avoid: Replacing the repaired user outcome with a Verified checklist alone.
- Still open: Benchmark method, competitive claims, customer revenue, actual repaired behavior and sound remain unverified.
