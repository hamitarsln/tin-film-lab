## Lessons

- Keep: Move from material detail to the full item in use.
- Try: For Tin event merchandise, open on stitching, show the garment worn and finish with the exact shop link.
- Try: Test a split view of fabric detail and full fit before the product range montage.
- Avoid: Announcing availability without a purchase destination.
- Still open: Actual sound, source methods, material specifications and buying details remain unverified.
