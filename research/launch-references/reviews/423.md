## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same brand details from website to kit to final asset.
- Try: Try a Tin split view with the extracted palette and final campaign poster visible together for three seconds.
- Try: Try a complete URL-to-asset sequence ending with a readable result, one edit and an export state.
- Avoid: Avoid replacing the first finished asset immediately after the loading state.
- Still open: Audio remains unheard; exact interpolation and the reliability of brand extraction are unverified.
