## Lessons

- Keep: Keep the same subject and motion while changing the option being compared.
- Try: For Tin ad variants, hold composition and subject constant while switching headline or brand palette.
- Try: Add a two-second photo-and-garment input view before the first outfit swap to test workflow understanding.
- Avoid: Presenting a polished result without the input or action that creates it.
- Still open: Actual product output provenance, exact transition mechanism, fit accuracy and sound remain unverified.
