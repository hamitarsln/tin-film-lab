## Lessons

- Keep: Show one preference at a time and carry its label into the recommended result.
- Try: Try a Tin brief with three questions, then an asset card tagged with those exact choices.
- Try: Try a same-scenario report after a user rejects one style, showing why the replacement fits.
- Avoid: Avoid changing the task just before its final result.
- Still open: Audio, exact card-entry interpolation and real recommendation quality remain unverified.
