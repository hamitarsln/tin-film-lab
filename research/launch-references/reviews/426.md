## Lessons

- Keep: Use a distinct accent color to show the exact mode being activated.
- Try: Try a Tin quick-edit sequence: request rounded card corners, enable mode, hold changed card.
- Try: Try a speed comparison only with labeled task, real elapsed times and equal start/end states.
- Avoid: Avoid a speed number without showing the requested result.
- Still open: Audio, true latency and offer applicability remain unverified; source title differs from visible speed claim.
