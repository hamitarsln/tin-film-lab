## Lessons

- Keep: Answer each community problem with a visible shared activity.
- Try: For a Tin workshop, pair no feedback with an actual review session and a revised campaign.
- Try: Replace a broad wealth metaphor with one participant’s finished launch and an application card.
- Avoid: Letting the venue price stand in for program value.
- Still open: Program terms, outcomes, income claims, footage provenance and sound remain unverified.
