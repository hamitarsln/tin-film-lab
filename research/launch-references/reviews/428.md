## Lessons

- Keep: Use a still, simple counter after a dense montage to focus the ending.
- Try: For Tin, show one campaign result before a labeled total such as campaigns delivered, with the time period.
- Try: Compare the generic achievement montage with real user work and a shared closing metric.
- Avoid: A large unlabeled number that relies on outside post copy for meaning.
- Still open: Metric definition, brand contribution, source rights and actual sound remain unverified.
