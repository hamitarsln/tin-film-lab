## Lessons

- Keep: Pair a physical action with a readable output beside the person.
- Try: For Tin, let a user say a deliberately rough campaign idea, then show the resulting draft and approval control.
- Try: Compare a shorter two-example comedy pitch with the longer monologue while keeping the same price ending.
- Avoid: Treating a floating post mockup as proof that content was published.
- Still open: Publication flow, price precision and actual audio delivery remain unverified.
