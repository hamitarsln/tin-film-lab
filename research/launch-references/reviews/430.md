## Lessons

- Keep: Carry an everyday metaphor through to the ending rather than abandoning it after the hook.
- Try: For Tin, show a familiar approval mistake, connect it to campaign permissions, then demonstrate the exact approval state.
- Try: Compare the current long comedy opening with a six-second version followed by one private-transaction example.
- Avoid: Using a relatable privacy metaphor as a substitute for showing the control.
- Still open: Funding, customer relationships and privacy implementation are not independently verified; sound remains unreviewed.
