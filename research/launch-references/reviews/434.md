# 434 · Runable | Introducing Runable: The General AI Agent for Every Task

**Product from the film:** Runable is presented as a general AI agent for reports, decks, websites and other tasks.

**Explains well:** A running-barrier metaphor connects personal ambition with the wish to remove routine work.

**Still unclear:** The film spends most of its time on motivation and frustration, then shows only brief prompts and output cards without a complete task.

## Lessons

- Keep: Return to the opening metaphor after the product section.
- Try: Use time saved from the history montage to show one complete market-report task.
- Try: For Tin, connect a physical obstacle metaphor to a specific automated campaign result.
- Avoid: Giving the emotional story far more evidence than the product payoff.
- Still open: Historical claims, product execution, asset provenance and audio remain unverified.
