## Lessons

- Keep: Link a repeated visual motif to the product name and interface palette.
- Try: Try a Tin opening that moves through one branded shape into the relevant workflow within five seconds.
- Try: Try an AI explanation demo with one source event and a three-second plain-language result hold.
- Avoid: Avoid making dense result text compete with a moving glowing background.
- Still open: Audio, sub-frame transition detail, AI accuracy and flash accessibility remain unchecked.
