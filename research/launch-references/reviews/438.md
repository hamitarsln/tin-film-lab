## Lessons

- Keep: Use a repeated prop and reaction structure to make a business comparison memorable.
- Try: For Tin, contrast one manually managed campaign with a clearly shown set of approved campaign outputs using the same brief.
- Try: Test a shorter reaction hold with a creator selection and brief screen inserted before the final slogan.
- Avoid: Claiming that a bigger creator pool alone proves better campaign results.
- Still open: Creator counts, cost and reach claims, service workflow and sound remain unverified.
