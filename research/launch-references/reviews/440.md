## Lessons

- Keep: Make the old identities visibly contribute to the new mark.
- Try: For Tin, combine a few recognizable workflow icons into one campaign overview, then show the actual overview screen.
- Try: Compare the symbol-only reveal with a final plain-language line describing the shared user benefit.
- Avoid: Assuming that familiar logos explain a new product structure to everyone.
- Still open: Product grouping and user-facing changes are not explained; other versions of the public post remain unverified.
