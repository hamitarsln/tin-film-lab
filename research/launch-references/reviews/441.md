## Lessons

- Keep: Use task-step labels in a wide view so the physical action remains traceable.
- Try: Keep autonomous, expert-assisted and future labels visible through each demonstration.
- Try: For Tin, show a complete campaign task with stable chapter markers and named action steps.
- Avoid: Mixing present, assisted and future capability footage without persistent context.
- Still open: Actual autonomy, control setup, reliability, safety, specifications, offer terms and audio remain unverified.
