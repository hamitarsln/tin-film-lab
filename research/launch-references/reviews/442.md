## Lessons

- Keep: Use an actual interaction surface to ground a broad AI positioning claim.
- Try: For Tin, place a readable approval conversation beside a presenter and follow it through to the exact campaign state.
- Try: Compare the provocative opening with a direct user-problem opening while keeping the same concrete message example.
- Avoid: Leaving the viewer unable to distinguish a product example from a visual joke.
- Still open: Interaction provenance, buyer and operator roles, autonomy claims and actual sound remain unverified.
