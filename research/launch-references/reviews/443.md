## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one visually distinct output through every stage of the workflow.
- Try: Try a Tin campaign video that follows one image from prompt through caption, approval and scheduled result.
- Try: Try a brief-to-published sequence with status labels and a final artifact hold, then show secondary capabilities.
- Avoid: Avoid burying the strongest complete task beneath unrelated examples and metaphors.
- Still open: Audio, automation reliability, benchmark conditions and exact UI cut timing remain unverified.
