## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one named deal through two people and several artifacts.
- Try: Trace one source assumption through a downside formula into the memo.
- Try: For Tin, preserve the same campaign name through a requester-to-creator handoff.
- Avoid: Treating a fast model montage as readable evidence of its calculations.
- Still open: Data/formula accuracy, connector behavior, financial conclusions and sound remain unverified.
