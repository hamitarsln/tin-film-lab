## Lessons

- Keep: Show both the work surface and the reward surface when introducing a creator service.
- Try: For Tin, follow one content task from brief to approved output before showing the performance total.
- Try: Compare the moving-phone montage with a stable close view of the same three key screens.
- Avoid: Letting payout imagery stand in for an explanation of the work required.
- Still open: Task rules, approval flow, payout completion and sound remain unverified.
