## Lessons

- Keep: Preview the next output in a card, then reveal that same setting at full size.
- Try: For Tin, select a campaign creative card and reveal the same creative in its destination placement.
- Try: Compare a shorter style montage plus one complete script-to-video step with the current example-heavy pitch.
- Avoid: Using polished examples and broad quality claims without a visible generation step.
