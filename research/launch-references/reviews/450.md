## Lessons

- Keep: Keep one output card fixed while changing its asset and color.
- Try: Show the creator submission status before the listing.
- Try: For Tin, compare asset and brand-color variants in one fixed campaign slot.
- Avoid: Skipping the outcome of a creator submission.
- Still open: Submission rules, licensing, live behavior and sound remain unverified.
