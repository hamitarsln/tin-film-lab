# 453 · Sorce | Sorce: Tinder for Jobs with AI Auto-Apply & Cover Letters

**Product from the film:** Sorce presents a mobile job-search app where a right swipe lets AI apply.

**Explains well:** The same job seeker moves from repeated application friction to a simpler phone flow and an interview invitation.

**Still unclear:** The application sent on the user’s behalf is never shown, so the viewer cannot see what is submitted or approved.

## Lessons

- Keep: Pair a clear failure state with a later desired result for the same person.
- Try: For Tin, show a rejected or stalled draft, then the exact edit and approval that produces a published result.
- Try: Compare the long frustration setup with a shorter opening plus an application-preview step.
- Avoid: Jumping from easy browsing to a desirable outcome without showing the work in between.
- Still open: Application content, approval controls, outcome provenance and sound remain unverified.
