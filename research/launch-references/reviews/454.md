## Lessons

- Keep: Let the product’s own window shape organize the visual story.
- Try: For Tin, use campaign panels to carry a short claim, then replace the text with an actual approved campaign result.
- Try: Compare the abstract work-life montage with one concrete browser task using the same cream visual style.
- Avoid: Broad ask-anything and do-anything claims with no example of either.
- Still open: Task behavior, availability details, exact motion timing and sound remain unverified.
