## Lessons

- Keep: Turn a two-part product promise into a simple visible diagram, then connect it to real task states.
- Try: Try a Tin opening that merges control and ease, followed immediately by one campaign brief and published artifact.
- Try: Try following one campaign through planning, a visible correction, approval and publication, with the same asset in each screen.
- Avoid: Avoid replacing a complete workflow with disconnected feature crops and unqualified numerical claims.
- Still open: Actual hearing, exact easing, benchmark conditions and product reliability remain unverified. The sample review does not establish phone readability.
