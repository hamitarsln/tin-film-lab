## Lessons

- Keep: Use a saved detail again in a later scene so memory has a visible payoff.
- Try: For Tin, save one customer insight and bring that exact detail back in a later campaign draft.
- Try: Add one short setup screen before the birthday reminder and compare whether its source becomes clearer.
- Avoid: Introducing contextual reminders without showing how their inputs were supplied.
- Still open: Reminder setup, privacy behavior, talking-point contents and sound remain unverified.
