## Lessons

- Keep: Reuse the film’s own character in the visible creation screens.
- Try: For Tin, show the launch film’s actual campaign brief and creative in its editor before returning to the finished ad.
- Try: Replace one metaphor scene with a real alert setup and received alert, keeping the same meme example.
- Avoid: Packing several creation stages into a montage without a readable input-to-export chain.
- Still open: Actual alert behavior, full production provenance, claimed cost, output controls and audio remain unverified.
