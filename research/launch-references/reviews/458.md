## Lessons

- Keep: Explain an abstract distinction with two simple physical props.
- Try: For Tin, compare a campaign plan card with the actual published asset, then show the publishing state.
- Try: Compare the sandwich metaphor alone with the metaphor followed by one complete generated-app action.
- Avoid: Letting an effective metaphor stand in for proof of the product claim.
- Still open: Funding, adoption figures, app functionality and actual sound remain unverified.
