# 460 · Nothing | Nothing raises $200M Series C at $1.3B valuation

**Product from the film:** Nothing presents a family of phones, earbuds and headphones with a shared industrial design.

**Explains well:** Repeated transparent parts, circular forms and white lighting make varied products feel like one recognizable family.

**Still unclear:** The fast retrospective does not clearly separate model names or explain what is newly available; the short software examples lack action labels.

## Lessons

- Keep: Unify a product family through repeated circular geometry and material details.
- Try: For Tin, give each campaign tool the same simple visual motif while showing one distinct task per tool.
- Try: Add a short Save this post label and a named destination to the shoe-post sequence.
- Avoid: Using rapid model changes without indicating which product or feature is new.
- Still open: Model chronology, capture gesture, source-title funding details, specifications and audio remain unverified.
