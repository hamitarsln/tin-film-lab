# 461 · Reve | Reimagine reality

**Product from the film:** Reve lets people change and explore images with plain-language requests.

**Explains well:** Several prompts stay beside the same image as a visible change appears, making the creative action easy to follow.

**Still unclear:** The lamp search does not show whether results are real purchasable products, while the projected-photo edit omits a clear command.

## Lessons

- Keep: Show a short request and preserve the subject so the requested change is easy to see.
- Try: For Tin, keep one ad layout fixed while a request changes only its headline or image treatment.
- Try: Compare the lamp sequence with a version that includes one real result card and its link.
- Avoid: Leaving a search result visually attractive but unclear about its real-world use.
- Still open: Exact photo edit mechanics, lamp sourcing, generation time, output consistency and audio remain unverified.
