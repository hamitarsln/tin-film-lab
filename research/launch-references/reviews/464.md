## Lessons

- Keep: Link each new topic to the previous question and return to the opening person.
- Try: Show one actual learning output after a topic prompt.
- Try: For Tin, connect a campaign uncertainty to guidance and a clear next action.
- Avoid: Letting rich subject imagery substitute for the product interaction.
- Still open: Output format, learning accuracy, factual script wording and sound remain uncertain.
