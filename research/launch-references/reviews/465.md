## Lessons

- Keep: Carry the same example business from generation into editing.
- Try: Pause the comedy inset while holding an article request and scheduled result.
- Try: For Tin, connect one source to one content request and one scheduled item.
- Avoid: Packing extra feature claims into a demo that has several competing focal points.
- Still open: Live functionality, deployment, scheduling, SEO results and sound remain unverified.
