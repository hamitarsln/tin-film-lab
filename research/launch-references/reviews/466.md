## Lessons

- Keep: Give installation and destination text their own uncluttered frames.
- Try: Show one named component working after installation.
- Try: For Tin, select one gallery item and hold its populated output.
- Avoid: Using a fast gallery as the only explanation of use.
- Still open: Installation, counts, compatibility, interaction behavior and sound remain unverified.
