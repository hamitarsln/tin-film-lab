## Lessons

- Keep: Use the buyer’s own specific question to organize the demo.
- Try: For Tin, ask how to approve a campaign and show the exact approval action before listing broader capabilities.
- Try: Compare the capability grid with one complete invoice walkthrough while retaining the instant-access opening.
- Avoid: Answering a concrete workflow question with a general promise.
- Still open: Live behavior, language coverage, sales impact and actual audio remain unverified.
