## Lessons

- Keep: Show the exact entry point and keep the assistant location consistent.
- Try: Hold one readable request, changed field and final status.
- Try: For Tin, show entry and one complete task before demonstrating range.
- Avoid: Treating a sequence of app pages as a completed workflow.
- Still open: Specific task outcomes, live behavior, permissions and sound remain unverified.
