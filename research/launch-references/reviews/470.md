## Lessons

- Keep: Use a recurring physical object to connect separate use-case stories.
- Try: For Tin, follow one approved campaign brief across creation, review and publishing, ending on a real customer result.
- Try: Compare three imagined benefits with one clearly labeled real task plus a short future-concept section.
- Avoid: Presenting dramatic interface effects without explaining their relationship to the real product.
- Still open: Actual hardware capabilities, available features, interface provenance and sound remain unverified.
