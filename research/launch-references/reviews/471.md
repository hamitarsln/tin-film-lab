## Lessons

- Keep: Carry a specific prompt detail into the output so the result feels connected.
- Try: For Tin, keep one requested product and campaign idea visible from prompt to finished creative.
- Try: Compare the paper-plane metaphor with a real share control followed by the same visual flourish.
- Avoid: Blurring the generated result with the launch film’s own visual effects.
- Still open: Output provenance, share completion, generation limits and actual sound remain unverified.
