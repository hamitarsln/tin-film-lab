## Lessons

- Keep: Use a concrete origin problem and let named team roles explain different operating needs.
- Try: Create a shorter funding cut with early news and a clear final action.
- Try: For Tin, connect each team role to one visible operation and attributable result.
- Avoid: Burying the announcement after several minutes when the intended format is a short launch update.
- Still open: Claims, caption accuracy, live app workflow and sound remain unverified; large ASR sections were unusable.
