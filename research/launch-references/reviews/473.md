# 473 · Tarun Amasa | It’s official. We’ve raised $14m led by @OpenAI Startup Fund to bring AI to Excel.

**Product from the film:** Endex is an Excel add-in that builds financial models and helps update existing workbooks with source-linked notes.

**Explains well:** The film shows both a blank-sheet build and an existing model, then opens a source document from the analysis.

**Still unclear:** The long full-window recording makes small changes hard to follow, and no single changed assumption is traced through its formula to a result.

## Lessons

- Keep: Open the supporting source from the exact result being discussed.
- Try: For Tin, select one campaign recommendation and open the source evidence that supports it.
- Try: Compare the long model-building view with three labeled checkpoints plus one assumption-to-result trace.
- Avoid: Treating a populated spreadsheet or visible citation as proof that every formula is correct.
- Still open: Model accuracy, complete formula linkage, data permissions, funding claims and actual audio remain unverified.
