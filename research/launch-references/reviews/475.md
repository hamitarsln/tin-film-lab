## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat the same request-to-result pattern on one dataset.
- Try: Show the share confirmation or recipient view.
- Try: For Tin, use one campaign dataset for three clearly labeled analysis views.
- Avoid: Leaving the final share action without a visible result.
- Still open: Calculations, data provenance, live execution, sharing and sound remain unverified.
