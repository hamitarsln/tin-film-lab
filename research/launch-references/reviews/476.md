## Lessons

- Keep: Preserve one meeting across the draft and native-app handoffs.
- Try: For Tin, carry one campaign name from brief to approval card to publishing confirmation.
- Try: Compare completion-style wording with Event ready to add before the native confirmation screen.
- Avoid: Claiming an action is complete while the visible workflow still needs user confirmation.
- Still open: Email delivery, saved-event persistence, exact transition timing and audio remain unverified.
