## Lessons

- Keep: Reveal the generated asset beside the source material.
- Try: For Tin, show an approved brief beside its generated campaign asset and check one required claim or brand rule.
- Try: Compare the raw tool-response section with three simple checkpoints: source, generation, editable result.
- Avoid: Calling a result branded without showing the brand inputs or checking them.
- Still open: Brand compliance, complete content transfer, generation timing and sound remain unverified.
