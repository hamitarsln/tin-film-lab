## Lessons

- Keep: Teach a hardware action through a repeated gesture and immediate visible confirmation.
- Try: For Tin, repeat the same approval action and status change across a few campaign types before showing broader workflow variety.
- Try: Compare the long poker scene with a shorter live moment followed by a saved-clip library view.
- Avoid: Making interface overlays look like a hardware display without explaining where they appear.
- Still open: Capture provenance, display location, streaming setup, hardware limits and actual sound remain unverified.
