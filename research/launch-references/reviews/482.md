## Lessons

- Keep: Show a real-looking source item flowing into one command and visible result.
- Try: For Tin, move one customer insight from a source card into a campaign command and output.
- Try: Add a readable amount and approval frame before the purchase status, then highlight one table row.
- Avoid: Showing a success state without enough of the preceding user decision to understand the action.
- Still open: Transaction reality, wallet setup, result accuracy, final outdoor task and audio remain unverified.
