# 483 · Cluely | Introducing Cluely for Customer Support

**Product from the film:** Cluely presents live assistance for customer-support agents through an exaggerated headset troubleshooting call.

**Explains well:** Suggestion and AI Response panels connect the agent’s conversation to visible assistance.

**Still unclear:** The comedy leaves unclear which diagnostic actions the agent performs and which actions the assistant can actually take.

## Lessons

- Keep: Alternate a readable suggestion with its effect on a real conversation.
- Try: For Tin, show a customer question, the suggested response and the marketer approving it before the customer result.
- Try: Compare this long sketch with a short version that keeps one diagnostic reveal and the final Wi-Fi fix.
- Avoid: Letting an extended joke obscure the user action and actual product boundary.
- Still open: Real diagnostic permissions, agent controls, exact spoken delivery and music remain unverified.
