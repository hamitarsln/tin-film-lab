## Lessons

- Keep: Reveal unexpected scale through a single backward camera move.
- Try: For Tin, begin on one campaign card and pull back to a full workspace while keeping the first card readable.
- Try: Replace one opaque assistance panel with a clear meeting question and answer before the pullback.
- Avoid: Depending on a topical name joke to explain a product benefit.
- Still open: Actual call assistance, simultaneous operation, identity-reference context and audio remain unverified.
