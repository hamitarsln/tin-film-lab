# 486 · Cluely | Introducing Cluely: Never Think Again

**Product from the film:** Cluely is a desktop assistant that overlays a call, offers notes, definitions and follow-up questions, and stores meeting summaries with sharing and personalization.

**Explains well:** The demo follows one call from Listen through live notes, definitions, a follow-up draft and a stored summary.

**Still unclear:** Personalization is introduced late through dense templates, but no later answer shows how a changed preference affects assistance.

## Lessons

- Keep: Trace one conversation detail through live help and post-call outputs.
- Try: Show how one saved preference changes a later answer.
- Try: For Tin, carry one campaign decision into a draft, action item and shared summary.
- Avoid: Treating a settings screen as proof that personalization improves the output.
- Still open: Recognition, latency, visibility to others, sharing behavior, personalization effects and sound remain unverified.
