## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat a simple identity pattern and finish with a group image.
- Try: For Tin, pair each team role with one concrete workflow responsibility instead of several personal metrics.
- Try: Compare this montage with a slower cut that gives each person one name, one role and one supporting image.
- Avoid: Packing several credentials into a shot too short to read.
- Still open: Product purpose, audience fit, credential accuracy and audio remain unverified.
