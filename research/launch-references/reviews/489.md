## Lessons

- Keep: Give an event invitation a simple social setup before presenting logistics.
- Try: For Tin, show one person missing a campaign discussion, then joining a clear shared workspace before the event invitation.
- Try: Add a static three-second event card after the fast words and compare recall of the date and invite path.
- Avoid: Hiding practical event information inside a fast word-by-word ending.
- Still open: Official event affiliation, location, invitation route and audio remain unverified.
