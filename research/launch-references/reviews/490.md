## Lessons

- Keep: Make the selected source visible before showing the research output.
- Try: For Tin, select customer feedback as a source, then show one resulting campaign insight with its source line.
- Try: Replace part of the report scroll with one chart value traced back to a filing passage.
- Avoid: Using dense charts and source lists as visual proof without connecting a specific claim to its source.
- Still open: Numerical accuracy, source coverage, plan availability, Spaces setup and audio remain unverified.
