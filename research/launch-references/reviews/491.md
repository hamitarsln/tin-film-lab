## Lessons

- Keep: Keep each request visibly matched to a distinct output type.
- Try: For Tin, show one campaign brief becoming a plan, creative draft and approval card with the same campaign name.
- Try: Add one user revision and export to the storyboard example, replacing part of the process-log scroll.
- Avoid: Treating a gallery of polished outputs as a complete account of how users finish their work.
- Still open: Artifact accuracy, sources, export behavior, revision controls, generation time and audio remain unverified.
