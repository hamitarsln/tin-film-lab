## Lessons

- Keep: Build the demo around connected follow-up questions rather than unrelated features.
- Try: For Tin, ask for campaign spend, then the channel split, then the largest performance risk using the same campaign.
- Try: Add a visible source account card and holdings chart beside the phone conversation.
- Avoid: Asking viewers to retain many financial figures without showing the underlying account view or a summary.
- Still open: Account data, source accuracy, live-demo integrity, privacy claims, voice quality and actual latency remain unverified.
