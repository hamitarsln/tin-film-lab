## Lessons

- Keep: Connect customer testimony to the customer’s own business and relevant screen.
- Try: For Tin, show one business owner describing a campaign problem, then their campaign draft and outcome with clearly scoped evidence.
- Try: Create a short customer-first cut that starts with ordering or marketing value and moves investor details to a separate funding version.
- Avoid: Combining funding, testimonials, founder advice, roadmap and hiring without a clear priority for the viewer.
- Still open: Customer-result attribution, future AI controls, feature availability and actual audio delivery remain unverified.
