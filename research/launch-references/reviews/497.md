## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one edited illustration through to its finished page.
- Try: For Tin, show one campaign illustration changing through three clear edits before placing it in the ad.
- Try: Add a short control label and a two-second before/after hold to the texture adjustment.
- Avoid: Showing many attractive effects without enough control context to explain how they are made.
- Still open: Exact control mechanics, timing, rendering method and audio remain unverified.
