## Lessons

- Keep: Pair the hiring offer with visible location, count, compensation and application route.
- Try: For Tin, show one team project during the office tour and name what a new hire would own.
- Try: Replace one growth-goal section with a day-in-the-role example and clear application criteria.
- Avoid: Using ambition and office scale as the main explanation of the work candidates would do.
- Still open: Hiring terms, revenue claims, role details, application status and audio remain unverified.
