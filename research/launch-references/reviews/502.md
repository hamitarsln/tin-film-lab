## Lessons

- Keep: Use clear interface states to make hidden assistance part of the visible story.
- Try: For Tin, show listening, drafting and approval states beside a campaign conversation with distinct readable labels.
- Try: Add a short actual meeting screen after the restaurant metaphor to explain the real usage surface.
- Avoid: Leaving the real product surface unclear after an extended metaphor.
- Still open: Actual device setup, data access, suggestion quality, live timing and audio remain unverified.
