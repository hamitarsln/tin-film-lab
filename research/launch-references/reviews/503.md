## Lessons

- Keep: Show the control and visible effect side by side.
- Try: For Tin, place a campaign control beside the exact headline or image region it changes.
- Try: Hold one before-and-after language response with the changed feature highlighted.
- Avoid: Letting a dramatic changed response imply a stronger scientific conclusion.
- Still open: Concept reliability, model setup, biological outcomes, live timing and heard sound remain unverified.
