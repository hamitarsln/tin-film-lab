## Lessons

- Keep: Connect the founder’s real-looking archive to one recognizable search result.
- Try: For Tin, show a messy asset folder before searching for a particular campaign image.
- Try: Use the retrieved bridge clip as the background for a readable product name and signup link.
- Avoid: Letting the origin story leave too little time for the product and destination.
- Still open: Import, indexing speed, exact clip identity, scale and audio remain unverified.
