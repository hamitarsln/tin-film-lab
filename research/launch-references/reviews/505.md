## Lessons

- Keep: Keep a recognizable sentence while changing its visual style.
- Try: For Tin, keep one headline fixed while cycling fonts so the design change is easy to compare.
- Try: Show one clear saved-entry state before the writer closes the laptop.
- Avoid: Implying an unexplained text-generation feature through a jump cut.
- Still open: Text origin, Chat behavior, save behavior, exact transitions and audio are unverified.
