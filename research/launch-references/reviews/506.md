## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat one recognizable control across the feature tour and visual climax.
- Try: For Tin, keep the same approval button visible through a workflow and show the actual resulting state before the brand ending.
- Try: Compare the long reveal with a version that shows the controller, a hand action and an app result in the opening five seconds.
- Avoid: Showing satisfying physical controls without their effect in the user’s software.
- Still open: Compatibility, setup, real control response and audio remain unverified.
