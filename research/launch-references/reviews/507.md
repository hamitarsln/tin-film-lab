## Lessons

- Keep: Give a company origin story one tangible analogy and a clear personal goal.
- Try: For Tin, use a short founder motivation story, then immediately show one customer campaign and the service delivered.
- Try: Compare this cut with a version that halves the historical analogy and uses the time for a client example.
- Avoid: Explaining the founder’s reason for starting while leaving the buyer’s reason to hire unclear.
- Still open: Service scope, customer deliverables, historical claims and audio remain unverified.
