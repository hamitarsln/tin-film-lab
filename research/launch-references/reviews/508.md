## Lessons

- Keep: Carry one recognizable image through the full multi-app task.
- Try: For Tin, keep the same campaign creative visible from generation through review and the final scheduled post.
- Try: Compare the demo with a short confirmation step showing recipient and selected image before completion.
- Avoid: Treating a completion label as proof of external receipt or broad automation reliability.
- Still open: Actual recipient receipt, approval behavior and general task reliability remain unverified; other versions of the public post are unverified.
