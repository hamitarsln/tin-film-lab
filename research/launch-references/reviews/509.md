## Lessons

- Keep: Use one recognizable object to connect capture, generation, selection and animation.
- Try: For Tin, photograph a package and carry that exact package through reference loading, creative selection and a final campaign asset.
- Try: Add a small availability label beside each demonstrated capability and a final available/coming-soon card.
- Avoid: Letting selected showcase clips stand in for a reproducible workflow or hiding release qualifications in closing speech.
- Still open: Generation latency, retries, full editing process, typical consistency, exact reflection and physics fidelity, and all heard sound remain unverified.
