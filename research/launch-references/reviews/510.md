## Lessons

- Keep: Make collaboration visible through shared cursors and one memorable group action.
- Try: For Tin, show collaborators reviewing one campaign, then a clear approval status that resolves the opening deadline.
- Try: Compare the final compatibility card with a short import example placed before the celebration.
- Avoid: Hiding a major practical feature after a closing sequence that feels complete.
- Still open: Import/export fidelity, actual performance and audio remain unverified.
