## Lessons

- Keep: Use a stable example and one highlighted change to explain precise editing.
- Try: For Tin, show one campaign sentence with only a selected product or audience detail changed, then its approved result.
- Try: Place a brief input-and-control demonstration before the charts so viewers know how the voice model is used.
- Avoid: Using waveform graphics as a substitute for demonstrating the actual editing control.
- Still open: Audible voice quality, accent fidelity, editing smoothness, benchmark validity and integration steps remain unverified.
