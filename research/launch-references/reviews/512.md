## Lessons

- Keep: Isolate physical controls before introducing output types.
- Try: Trace one recorded sentence into its matching action item.
- Try: For Tin, carry one meeting decision into the same named campaign task.
- Avoid: Letting generic output cards imply a demonstrated recording-to-summary result.
- Still open: Recording accuracy, specifications, privacy, current offer and sound remain unverified.
