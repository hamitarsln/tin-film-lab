## Lessons

- Keep: Pair the call with the current instruction and branch.
- Try: Show one integration through a final calendar or record result.
- Try: For Tin, connect one agent request to a visible branch and final output.
- Avoid: Treating the agent’s description of integrations as evidence they executed.
- Still open: Voice quality, latency, live pathway sync, integrations and broad business claims remain unverified.
