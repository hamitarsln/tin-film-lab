# 514 · Friend | Introducing Friend. Not Imaginary.

**Product from the film:** Friend is a wearable pendant companion that sends named, context-related messages to a phone.

**Explains well:** The same pendant-to-phone message pattern appears during a walk, gaming and a meal, making companionship concrete.

**Still unclear:** The film does not explain when the pendant listens, when a touch is needed, or how a conversation becomes a phone reply.

## Lessons

- Keep: Repeat the pendant, named reply and human reaction across several settings.
- Try: Show one complete trigger-to-reply interaction with a clear listening boundary.
- Try: For Tin, use a recognizable result card across different campaign tasks.
- Avoid: Letting lifestyle scenes carry the entire explanation of an unfamiliar interaction.
- Still open: Listening rules, privacy, generated replies, voice and music remain unverified.
