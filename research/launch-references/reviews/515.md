# 515 · supermemory | supermemory: AI Second Brain for Bookmarks, Tweets & Notes

**Product from the film:** Supermemory lets users ask questions about saved internet content, organize it into spaces and add material through several entry points.

**Explains well:** The film moves from a saved-content grid to a suggested question and an answer with source cards.

**Still unclear:** The selected source opens to a loading X page, so the viewer never gets a clear comparison between the answer and the original post.

## Lessons

- Keep: Show saved material, a concrete question and source-linked answer in one short sequence.
- Try: For Tin, retrieve a saved brand rule, show its source text and then the campaign sentence that follows it.
- Try: Compare the current split screen with a full-width answer and source view while the presenter becomes a small corner inset.
- Avoid: Ending a source-verification step on a loading screen.
- Still open: Source grounding, import completeness, entry-point behavior, metrics and audio remain unverified.
