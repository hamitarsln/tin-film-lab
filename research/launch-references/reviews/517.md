## Lessons

- Keep: Keep one inquiry continuous through questions, answer and a visual follow-up.
- Try: Show the entry step that connects the image inquiry to video results.
- Try: For Tin, carry one campaign brief through choices, generated work and a final preview.
- Avoid: Switching to a new query without showing the action that starts it.
- Still open: No local speech draft was present; audio remains unassessed rather than absent. Accuracy, speed and media provenance are unverified.
